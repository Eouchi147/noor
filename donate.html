What it actually costs to keep this open

The servers and the delivery networkevery month
The Qur’an text and recitation servicesevery month
The domain names and their protectionevery year
Storage, email, backups and the tools that build the roomsevery month
The hours: writing, checking every reference, fixing what breaksevery day

None of this is paid by advertising, by selling anything, or by anyone’s institution. One person carries it, out of his own pocket, and has decided to keep carrying it for as long as he is able. That is a promise, not a complaint. But every gift moves the day further away when the choice becomes hard.

✦Builds new rooms. The Mushaf, the 25 Prophets, the Kids' Codex, the companions: every room was built free. Your gift builds the next one.
✦Carries the light further. The Codex is preparing to speak every major language of the ummah. Gifts bring that day closer.
✦Gives onward. As a personal commitment of the keeper, 2.5% of money received is set aside for zakat-eligible causes (9:60), and 17.5% toward a fund intended to help build masajid where they are needed. A chosen discipline of the house, told in full in the open ledger; not a condition attached to your Gift.

Is this zakat? Your gift itself is sadaqa, voluntary charity, not zakat, and we are not a registered charity, so no tax receipt is issued. Direct your own zakat to the eight categories named in the Qur'an (9:60). And know that from every gift received here, 2.5% is set aside and given onward to zakat-eligible causes.
