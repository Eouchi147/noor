Two Lives in True Scale

Not poetry: measurements. What the Qur'an and the authentic Sunnah state, in numbers, about the life you are in and the one you are heading to.

The Finger and the Sea

"By Allah, this world beside the Hereafter is nothing but as when one of you dips this finger of his into the sea: so let him see what it brings back."

Muslim 2858
the drop : the sea

Everything ever owned, built, or won by every human in history fits in the droplet. The sea is what remains.

The Dip That Resets Memory

The most comfortable denier who ever lived one dip in the Fire:

"O son of Adam, have you ever seen any good? He says: No, by Allah, my Lord."

The most afflicted believer who ever lived one dip in Jannah:

"O son of Adam, have you ever seen any hardship? He says: No, by Allah, my Lord."

Muslim 2807

One immersion erases the entire memory of seventy years. That is the actual weight of every luxury envied and every hardship feared here.

Two Capitals, Spent Blind

+Healthworks only while it lasts
Free timeleaves without notice

"Two blessings about which many people are cheated: health and free time."

Bukhari 6412

The only two currencies any deed is ever bought with. Both are draining while you read this sentence, and the next life pays out exactly what they purchased.

Nothing Here Hurts for Free

"No fatigue, nor disease, nor sorrow, nor sadness, nor hurt, nor distress befalls a Muslim, even the prick he receives from a thorn, but Allah expiates some of his sins for it."

Bukhari 5641

"No Muslim is afflicted by any harm but Allah sheds his sins as a tree sheds its leaves."

Bukhari 5660 · Muslim 2571

Here, pain subtracts from what stands against you: every fever, worry, and thorn quietly lightens the load. That exchange runs only on this side of the grave.

Shade, on the Day There Is None

"Seven, Allah will shade them in His shade on the Day when there is no shade but His."

Bukhari 660 · Muslim 1031
١A just leader
٢A youth raised in worship
٣A heart hung on the mosques
٤Two who love each other for Allah alone
٥One who is called by beauty and answers: I fear Allah
٦A hand that gives so secretly the left does not know
٧Eyes that weep alone, remembering Allah

Fifty thousand years of sun, and shade is bought here, now, mostly for free: none of the seven requires wealth, and every one of them is available today.

The Rider and the Tree

"What have I to do with this world? I and this world are but as a rider who shades himself under a tree, then moves on and leaves it."

Tirmidhi 2377

"Be in this world as if you were a stranger, or a traveler on the road."

Bukhari 6416

If this world weighed as much as a mosquito's wing with Allah, He would not have given a denier a sip of its water (Tirmidhi 2320). It weighs less; so travel light, and load the mount with what the next country accepts.
