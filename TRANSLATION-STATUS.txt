How it works, so a later session can pick it up without redoing anything:

  python3 scripts/i18n.py status        what every language has, measured
  python3 scripts/i18n.py todo <code>   the next batch of untranslated strings
  python3 scripts/i18n.py merge <code>  fold finished parts back into the pack

Seven packs are not in this zip: ha, so, ku, sw, zh, ja and ko. They contain only
strings that need no translator at all, hadith references and numbers, so shipping
them would change nothing a reader sees. Their menus and their gateway pages are
fully translated and those ARE in this zip.

Nothing already translated is ever translated twice. The queue is keyed on the
string itself, so it survives across sessions.
