A word of the Path

Tabi'i التَّابِعِيّ

Editorial

A Muslim of the generation after the Companions, who met a Companion but not the Prophet.

What does Tabi'i mean in Islam?

The plural is tabi'un or tabi'in. They form the second layer in almost every chain, and figures like Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, al-Hasan al-Basri and Ibn Sirin belong here. Their own statements are called maqtu', and their reports about the Prophet without naming the Companion in between are called mursal.
