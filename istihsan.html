A word of the Path

Istihsan اِسْتِحْسان

Scholars differ

Juristic preference: setting aside a strict analogy for a stronger reason of fairness or need.

What does Istihsan mean in Islam?

It is chiefly a Hanafi and Maliki tool, used where the obvious qiyas would cause real hardship or would collide with a text, a consensus or plain necessity. The Hanafi allowance of manufacturing contracts, where the goods do not yet exist, is a standard example. Al-Shafi'i attacked istihsan in famous words, saying whoever practises it has made law himself, though his objection was to arbitrary preference rather than to reasoned exception.
