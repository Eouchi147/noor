2 · A PICTURE OF ITS OWN FOR EVERY POST     (api/_art.js, card.js, _lights.js)
=========================================================================
  Twelve motifs out of the Islamic geometric tradition — a rosette, the
  eightfold seal, the lamp and its niche, an arch, strapwork, a hexagon field,
  ripples, rays, a crescent, a sky, open leaves. Each card gets a large faint
  watermark and a small crisp emblem, both a pure function of the card's own
  words, so one card is always drawn the same way and two are not drawn alike.

  THE LANTERN CHOOSES; IT DOES NOT DRAW. It is handed the twelve names and
  what each is for, and it answers with one — and that answer is checked
  against the list before anything is used. A model that could put arbitrary
  SVG on a card published in your name could put anything there: a figure, a
  face, the symbol of another faith. Choosing from a vetted vocabulary means
  there are twelve drawings on this site and a person can look at all twelve.

  If the Lantern is dark, the motif is read off the card's own words instead.

  tests/art.mjs renders every motif at many seeds and reads the geometry of
  every path for a horizontal stroke crossing a vertical one — the same test
  the site-wide symbol guard runs, applied to art that only exists at run
  time. 72 rendered samples, 0 crosses. The emblem is also proved to sit
  above the title on the tightest possible card, so it can never land on the
  words.

4 · THE DIAL IS BACK, IN THE CONSOLE       (assets/noor-console-dial.js)
=========================================================================
  It is not a ring. A ring of twelve cannot fit a wide, short strip and no
  tuning of the numbers makes it: sections spaced evenly in angle bunch at the
  ends, so twelve of them need about 600px of height. The console does not
  have 600px to give its own navigation.

  So it is a shallow ARC — a tuning dial, which is what the word means. Drag
  it, spin the wheel, use the arrows, or tap. Tap the section in the middle to
  open it. It drives the tabs that were already there, so the list down the
  side and the keyboard shortcuts keep working, and with this file missing the
  console is exactly what it was. That last one is a test.

  Everything the public dial got wrong is a test here, because all of it was
  found in production rather than in a test: the tap that did nothing, the
  overlap, the pile-up, the square glow.
