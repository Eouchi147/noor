Ask the Lantern

The Codex's resident guide. Ask anything about Islam: a verse, a story, a practice, a doubt. It answers from the Qur'an and authenticated hadith, tells you plainly when it is unsure, and points you to a scholar when a question deserves one.

The Lantern is a guide, not a mufti, and it can err. For personal rulings, ask a qualified scholar or your local imam. Questions are sent only to the Codex's own server to be answered, never sold, never used to track you.

The Lantern's standards · answers grounded in the Qur'an and authenticated hadith, cited precisely · the mainstream of Sunni scholarship first, honest about where the schools differ · no fatwas, ever: personal rulings belong with qualified scholars · uncertainty admitted plainly · and on matters of interpretation, Allah knows best.
