A word of the Path

Basmalah بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحيم

Scholars differ

The opening line In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, found before nearly every surah.

What does Basmalah mean in Islam?

It stands at the head of 113 surahs; at-Tawbah alone has none. It also appears inside the Qur'an as the opening of Sulayman's letter in Surah an-Naml. The schools differ over whether it is a verse of al-Fatihah and whether it is said aloud in prayer, which is why practice varies from one mosque to the next.
