ن نُور
The Path Nihaya Companions

أَصْحَابُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ

Companions of the Messenger

Abstract seals only. No faces. Each seal carries a title and a virtue. The Companions are remembered by light, names, and the marks they left on the Path.

الصِّدِّيق
As-Siddiq

Abu Bakr as-Siddiq

أبو بكر الصديق

First adult male to believe. Companion of the Cave. First Caliph. His truthfulness never wavered — even on the morning after the Isra.

الفَارُوق
Al-Faruq

Umar ibn al-Khattab

عمر بن الخطاب

The one who distinguished truth from falsehood. Second Caliph. Justice was his hallmark. The lands of the empires began to open under him.

ذُو النُّورَيْن
Dhun-Nurayn

Uthman ibn Affan

عثمان بن عفان

Possessor of the Two Lights. Third Caliph. Compiled the Qur’an into a single official mushaf. Extreme modesty and generosity.

أَبُو تُرَاب
Abu Turab

Ali ibn Abi Talib

علي بن أبي طالب

Cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet. First child to believe. Fourth Caliph. Gate of the city of knowledge. Courage and knowledge combined.

أَسَدُ اللَّه
Asadullah

Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib

حمزة بن عبد المطلب

Lion of Allah and of His Messenger. Uncle of the Prophet. Early convert. Martyred at Uhud while defending the Messenger.

مُؤَذِّن
Mu’adhdhin

Bilal ibn Rabah

بلال بن رباح

First mu’adhdhin of Islam. Former slave tortured for his faith. His voice called the adhan from the Kaaba on the day of the Opening of Makkah.

الفَارِسِيّ
Al-Farisi

Salman al-Farisi

سلمان الفارسي

The seeker who crossed empires looking for the true prophet. Suggested the trench at Khandaq. The Prophet said: “Salman is one of us, the Ahl al-Bayt.”

سَيْفُ اللَّه
Sayfullah

Khalid ibn al-Walid

خالد بن الوليد

The Sword of Allah. Never defeated in battle after accepting Islam. Instrumental at Mu’tah, the Opening, and the wars of apostasy.

أُمُّ المُؤْمِنِين
Umm al-Mu’minin

Khadijah bint Khuwaylid

خديجة بنت خويلد

First to believe. The Prophet’s first wife and strongest supporter. When the first revelation came, she covered him and believed without hesitation.

سَيِّدَةُ نِسَاء
Sayyidat Nisa al-Alamin

Fatimah bint Muhammad

فاطمة بنت محمد

Daughter of the Prophet, wife of Ali, mother of Hasan and Husayn. Part of the Prophet — the one whose pleasure is the pleasure of Allah.

أُمُّ المُؤْمِنِين
Umm al-Mu’minin

Aisha bint Abi Bakr

عائشة بنت أبي بكر

Mother of the Believers. The most knowledgeable of the women of the ummah. Many rulings and details of the Sunnah were preserved through her.

أَمِينُ الأُمَّة
Amin al-Ummah

Abu Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah

أبو عبيدة بن الجراح

The Trustworthy of the Ummah. One of the ten promised Paradise. Led the armies into Syria. Known for humility and reliability.

More seals will be added over time. Prophets remain represented only by light, seals, and calligraphy — never faces.
