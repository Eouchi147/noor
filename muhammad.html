وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةً لِّلْعَالَمِينَ

Qur'an 21:107

Twenty-Three Years

From a cave above Makkah to a grave in Madinah, and then the harder question: what he was actually like, and how a person is supposed to copy it. Every verse and every narration below is carried with its source.

Walk it

The shape of it

Thirteen years of being refused, ten of building

Prophethood lasted twenty-three years and it was not one thing twice. Thirteen were spent in Makkah with almost no power and almost no protection. Ten were spent in Madinah with both. The first period is where most of the Qur'an about patience came down; the second is where almost all of the law did.

The road · twenty stations

Each one carries its own evidence

Every station names the verse and the narration it rests on. Nothing here is summary without a source behind it.

    Badr · 17 Ramadan, 2 AH

    Three hundred and thirteen

    The numbers are the argument. Every dot on the right is one man. The believers came out with no intention of a battle and were given one anyway, against an army more than three times their size, better armed and better mounted.

    Allah had already given you victory at Badr while you were few. Qur'an 3:123

    313 believers about 1,000 of Quraysh

    The example · al-uswa al-hasana

    He was sent to be copied, not only believed

    The Qur'an does not describe him as a set of doctrines to agree with. It calls him a pattern, and the word it uses is the one for a mould a thing is pressed into. Everything below is here because a person can do it tomorrow.

    There is certainly in the Messenger of Allah an excellent pattern for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day, and remembers Allah much. Qur'an 33:21

    What the people who lived with him said

    The method · how he handled things

    Nine problems, and what he actually did

    Not maxims. Real situations with a person in front of him and a decision to make. Read the problem, then open it to see what he did and the rule underneath, which is the part that still works on a Tuesday.

    The day · his habits, in the order they fell

    A life with a shape to it

    He did not live in bursts. The day had joints in it, and the same small acts sat at the same joints for twenty-three years. That repetition is most of what made the character.

    Muhammad is only a messenger; messengers have passed away before him. If he dies or is killed, will you turn back on your heels? Qur'an 3:144
