Ulum al-Qur'an عُلومُ القُرْآن

Editorial

The family of disciplines that study how the Qur'an was revealed, collected, recited, read and interpreted.

What does Ulum al-Qur'an mean in Islam?

It covers revelation and its occasions, Makkan and Madinan passages, the canonical readings, abrogation, the clear and the ambiguous, and the rules of interpretation. It is the map you are meant to have before opening a work of tafsir. The two classic reference works are al-Zarkashi's al-Burhan and al-Suyuti's al-Itqan.
