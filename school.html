الْمَدْرَسَة

The Madrasa of Light · A Complete School

From the cradle to the edge of adulthood: a full curriculum that joins the prophetic way of teaching, the Montessori respect for the child, and the scientific spirit that once made Muslims the teachers of the world. Free for every family. Ready for every classroom.

5 stages · 0 to 18 6 subject tracks Montessori-informed STEM · golden age Ijazah milestones

١ Three springs, one river

Every lesson in this Madrasa drinks from three springs at once. None contradicts the others; together they make a child who loves Allah, loves learning, and can do things with their own two hands.

🕌The Prophetic Way

The Prophet ﷺ taught with gentleness, stories, questions, and perfect timing: prayer is invited at seven, expected at ten; mercy for the young is a condition of the community, not a decoration.

“Make things easy and do not make them difficult; give glad tidings and do not repel.” · Bukhari 69
🌱The Montessori Respect

“Help me do it myself.” The child is not an empty cup but a builder. Prepared environment, freedom within limits, concrete before abstract, self-correcting materials, and long unhurried work cycles.

Planes of development · sensitive periods · practical life
🔭The Scientific Spirit

Observe, question, test, record: the method written down by Ibn al-Haytham a thousand years ago. STEM here is not imported; it is inherited. The Qur'an commands looking at camels, skies, and seeds.

Observe → ask → test → record → share

يَا بُنَيَّ أَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ وَأْمُرْ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَانْهَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ وَاصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا أَصَابَكَ

Luqman teaching his son: “My dear son, establish the prayer, enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and be patient with what befalls you.” The Qur'an's own model of a parent-teacher: tender address, clear content, realistic about difficulty.

Luqman 31:17
“Command your children to pray at seven, and insist on it at ten.” Stage-appropriate expectations, centuries before developmental psychology named them.· Abu Dawud 495
“He is not one of us who does not show mercy to our young and respect to our elders.”· Tirmidhi 1919

٣ The Observatory of Signs · STEM

Science is not an import into this school; it is a homecoming. The Qur'an points at the world and says look. For centuries, Muslims looked hardest of all. This track hands the child that telescope back.

إِنَّ فِي خَلْقِ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَاخْتِلَافِ اللَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ لَآيَاتٍ لِّأُولِي الْأَلْبَابِ

“In the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of night and day are signs for people of understanding.” The Madrasa's science motto.

Aal Imran 3:190

أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى الْإِبِلِ كَيْفَ خُلِقَتْ ۝ وَإِلَى السَّمَاءِ كَيْفَ رُفِعَتْ

“Do they not look at the camel, how it was created? And at the sky, how it was raised?” Observation is commanded, not permitted.

Al-Ghashiyah 88:17-18

الشَّمْسُ وَالْقَمَرُ بِحُسْبَانٍ

“The sun and the moon move by precise calculation.” The ayah every moon journal and prayer-time computation sits under.

Ar-Rahman 55:5

قُلْ سِيرُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ فَانظُرُوا كَيْفَ بَدَأَ الْخَلْقَ

“Say: travel through the earth and observe how He began creation.” Fieldwork, geology, biology: commanded travel with open eyes.

Al-Ankabut 29:20

The teachers of the world · study one, become one

Nine real people, no legends added. In the Observatory stage each learner adopts one, reads their life, rebuilds a piece of their work, and presents them to the class in first person.

965 to 1040 · Basra & CairoIbn al-Haytham

Wrote the Book of Optics; proved sight is light entering the eye; tested claims with controlled experiments and darkened rooms: the camera obscura. Called the father of the scientific method.

OPTICS · METHOD
~780 to 850 · BaghdadAl-Khwarizmi

His book al-Jabr named algebra; his name, Latinized, became “algorithm.” Brought the Hindu-Arabic numerals westward: the digits the whole world now writes.

MATH · ALGORITHMS
d. 880 · FezFatima al-Fihri

Spent her inheritance to found al-Qarawiyyin in 859 CE, recognized as the oldest continuously operating degree-granting university on earth. A woman built the world's first university, and it still teaches.

EDUCATION
980 to 1037 · Bukhara & PersiaIbn Sina

His Canon of Medicine organized all known medicine into one system and taught European universities for some six centuries. Philosopher, physician, polymath.

MEDICINE
936 to 1013 · CordobaAl-Zahrawi

Wrote al-Tasrif, thirty volumes of medicine and surgery; illustrated some 200 surgical instruments, many of which he devised; used catgut for internal stitches, still used today.

SURGERY
10th century · AleppoMariam al-Astrulabi

Renowned maker of astrolabes, the pocket universe that found the qibla, the time, and the stars. Precision engineering in a woman's hands, a thousand years ago.

ASTRONOMY · CRAFT
973 to 1048 · Khwarazm & GhazniAl-Biruni

Measured the Earth's radius from a mountain and a horizon with astonishing accuracy; compared calendars and cultures like a modern anthropologist.

GEODESY
1136 to 1206 · DiyarbakirAl-Jazari

His Book of Ingenious Mechanical Devices described water clocks, pumps, and automata, with the crank-connecting rod system later at the heart of every engine.

ENGINEERING
1304 to 1369 · Tangier to ChinaIbn Battuta

Left for Hajj at 21 and kept going: some 29 years and around 117,000 km across Africa and Asia, dictated as the Rihla. Geography learned with the feet.

GEOGRAPHY

The project shelf · hands before textbooks

Every project is cheap, safe, and self-correcting: the Montessori control of error is built in, because the sky does not lie and the shadow does not flatter.

🌙The Moon Journal

Draw the moon every clear night for a month. Discover the 29/30-day rhythm and why Ramadan moves through the seasons.

Workshop 6-9
🌞The Shadow Clock

A stick, chalk, and a sunny day: mark the shadow at each prayer. Watch Dhuhr shrink it and Asr stretch it: prayer times made visible.

Workshop 6-9
📷Ibn al-Haytham's Box

A shoebox, foil, a pinhole, tracing paper: the camera obscura. The image lands upside down; ask why, then read how he answered.

Workshop → Observatory
✴️Girih Star Lab

Compass and straightedge to an eight-fold star like the mosque tiles. Geometry, symmetry, and why the pattern never ends.

Observatory 9-12
⚙️Al-Jazari's Wheel

Cardboard, skewers, cups and string: a water-lifting wheel with a working crank. The part every engine still uses.

Observatory 9-12
🧭Qibla Two Ways

Find Makkah by the sun's shadow at solar noon, then by the stars; check with a compass last. Trust, verify, understand.

Observatory 9-12
🌱The Seed Ledger

Plant seven beans, give charity-water to some and none to others (then rescue all). Chart growth: a fair test, and 2:261's seven ears remembered.

Garden → Workshop
🧮Zakat of the Toy Shelf

Count, value, compute 2.5%, and actually give it. Percentages with a soul.

Workshop 6-9
⏰Compute Fajr

Academy capstone: from latitude, longitude and solar equations to a real prayer timetable, verified against the local masjid.

Academy 12-18

٤ Practical life is worship

Montessori's deepest shelf, and Islam's oldest one. The sunnah is a practical-life curriculum: how to eat, greet, wash, dress, sleep, and serve. Mastery here is mastery of the day itself.

💧Care of self

Wudu in order · miswak or brushing · right shoe first · sleeping on the right side with the sleep adhkar · dressing with the du'a of clothing.

each skill: shown slowly, practiced freely, never rushed
🏠Care of the home

Fold the prayer mats · straighten the shoes at the door · water the plants · return the Mushaf to the highest shelf · help set and clear the sufra.

“He ﷺ was in the service of his family” · Bukhari 676
🕊Grace & courtesy

Salam first · stand for elders lightly · pass with the right hand · knock and wait three times · the guest's plate is filled first.

adab lessons given as tiny theater, then lived
🤲Care of others

The sadaqa jar with a family ledger · a plate to the neighbor while it is warm · visiting the sick as a family outing · feeding birds in winter.

service scheduled weekly, like mathematics
