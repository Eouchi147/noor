# The Lantern: one right hand for the owner

Owner's words (3 October 2026): "I am the owner and CEO of this project and the Soul/Lantern is my right hand, doing everything, thinking towards the goals, actually taking concrete action and steps autonomously. I need to have a place where I can see what is going on, what has been done, what needs to be done and what should be done. I am the main decision maker but the AI will take care of everything else. It needs to have the freedom and power to do so."

Until now the house had three voices: the Steward (operational findings on Today), the Lantern (the chat agent) and the Soul (the daily cycle). From now on there is ONE entity, called **the Lantern**, with one memory, one record of actions, one list of goals, one council and one place where the owner meets it: the console's Home.

Code keeps its file names (api/_soul.js, api/_mind.js, api/soul.js, api/lantern-agent.js, api/_steward.js); the word "Soul" disappears from everything the owner reads. SOUL.md stays the reference for the constitution, tiers, council, cycle and instruments; this file adds the unification, the owner's Home and the new levers.

## 1. The owner's Home (admin2.html, the first screen after unlocking)

Phone first (390 px), one scroll, in this order:

1. **Greeting and status**: "Assalamu alaykum", today's Hijri and Gregorian date, a status chip (The Lantern is working / Paused / Needs you), a small Pause control.
2. **The brief**: what the Lantern wrote this morning, in 3 to 5 plain sentences (what happened, where we stand against the goals, what it did, what it will do), with three key numbers (people reached this week with the change against last week; site visitors this week; share of each reel watched) and "Open the week's scorecard".
3. **Your decisions**: only what needs the owner, each a card with a title, one line of why, the goal it serves, and buttons (Yes / No / Later, or named choices, or "Open" plus short steps for something only he can do outside the house). Empty state: "Nothing needs you. The Lantern has it."
4. **Done**: what the Lantern and the machine did in the last 3 days, newest first, each with Undo where an undo exists, plus the routine ("10 of 10 posts went out yesterday"). "See everything" opens the full record.
5. **Next**: what the Lantern plans to do (today, tomorrow, this week), each with "Do it now" and "Skip"; plus what is coming on the calendar (the Monday scorecard, a test's end). A button "Think again" runs a cycle now.
6. **Goals**: each goal with a progress bar, current value against target, an on-track / behind / met chip, the projection ("on track to reach 10,600 by 25 December"), and one line on what the Lantern is doing about it.
7. **Ideas to grow**: bigger moves the Lantern recommends, each with why, expected effect and who does it (the Lantern itself, a build for Claude, or the owner), and "Go" / "Not now" / "Never".
8. **Ask the Lantern**: a text box that sends straight to the conversation (the Ask tab), with three suggested orders.
9. A quiet link to **The engine room** (audit, playbook, budget, Telegram link, instruments, cycle details: today's Soul room renamed).

Bottom bar, five buttons: **Home**, **Ask** (the Lantern conversation), **Posts** (the line-up, with today's reels to share by hand), **Numbers** (the Observatory, readers and the scorecard), **More** (every other room, the engine room first).

## 2. The Home contract (api/soul.js)

**As built (3 October 2026).** The shape below holds field for field. What the build settled, beside it:
- One more top-level field, `missing`: `{part: reason}` for each part that could not be read (that part is null). A part with nothing in it is empty, not missing: before any cycle has written a brief, `brief` is null with no reason.
- Each part is read on its own clock (6 seconds) from the store and caches only; no model is asked, so the answer comes in well under 10 seconds.
- `status` is "needs-you" whenever a decision is showing (and not paused). `goals[].owner` is "owner" or "lantern"; `goals[].status` is met, on-track, behind or no-data. A unit (`goals[].unit`, `brief.numbers[].unit`) is one of people, views, visitors, share (a fraction of one), percent (already times 100), seconds, posts, usd, lessons. `brief.numbers` is always reach (people), visitors (visitors) and watched (percent, its delta in points); `deltaUnit` names the delta's unit.
- The option with id "open" changes nothing on the server: the console draws it as the card's link and need not post it. Every other option is posted as {action:"decide", id, option}; one with `confirm` asks that line first. A link is a console room (`/admin2#posts`, `#readers`, `#system`, `#observatory`, `#lantern`, `#night`, or `#soul`, which is the engine room) or an https address.
- `next[].id` is "q-..." for a step waiting in the queue, "i:<cycle id>:<n>" for a step of a cycle. `canDoNow` is true on a blocked step too: his approval may run what the council refused, and the red lines and caps still hold.
- `done[]` carries one routine line for each of the two days before today (id "routine:<date>", by "machine", undo false); today's own count is `today.posts`.
- Every POST the Home sends answers {ok, message} with HTTP 200, the message in plain words and ready for a toast; a refusal is ok:false with its message (only an undo of an unknown action id answers 404, and an unknown action 400). decide also answers `executed` and `actionId` when a hand ran, `until` for Later and `link` for an open. "Think again" is POST {action:"run"}.

GET /api/soul?view=home (owner gate) answers in one call, every part failing soft on its own (null plus a reason):

```
{ ok, now, name: "the Lantern", paused, status: "working"|"paused"|"needs-you",
  brief: { date, text, numbers: [{ key, label, value, unit, delta, deltaUnit }], at, cycle } | null,
  decisions: [{ id, kind: "approve"|"choose"|"you"|"build", title, why, goal, impact,
    options: [{ id, label, style: "primary"|"plain"|"danger", confirm }], link: { href, label } | null,
    steps: [string], at, expires }],
  done: [{ id, at, title, detail, goal, by: "lantern"|"owner"|"machine", ok, undo: boolean, actionId }],
  next: [{ id, title, why, goal, when: "today"|"tomorrow"|"this week"|date, hand, tier, status: "planned"|"blocked", reason, canDoNow, canSkip }],
  coming: [{ date, title }],
  goals: [{ id, owner, outcome, metric, unit, baseline, current, target, due, status, projected, eta, note, focus }],
  ideas: [{ id, title, why, impact, who: "lantern"|"build"|"you", status: "new"|"go"|"later"|"never", at }],
  today: { posts: { sent, due, failed }, fixed, reach7: { value, delta } },
  voice: { telegram: { linked } }, spend: { usd, capUsd } }
```

POST bodies (owner gate, each answers {ok, message} in plain words and never throws):
- {action:"decide", id, option}: carries out the chosen option (section 3).
- {action:"do-now", id}: runs a Next item now with the owner's approval (section 4).
- {action:"skip", id}: drops a Next item; the Lantern does not plan it again for 7 days.
- {action:"idea", id, choice:"go"|"later"|"never"}.
- {action:"undo", id} as today (id is the action id).
- {action:"pause"|"resume"|"run"} as today.

## 3. Decisions

One store, nsoul:decisions (open ones, at most 12; decided ones archived 30 days), deduplicated by a stable key so the same need never appears twice. Sources:
- the cycle's needs (link Telegram, add OpenRouter credit, Google Search Console access, a goal behind for 7 days, a test verdict to act on): kind "you" with a link and steps, or "choose";
- the conversation's proposals (a line-up change, a test to plan): kind "approve";
- upgrade proposals ("Build this?"): kind "build";
- inbox messages and journal replies waiting, folded from the Steward: kind "you" with "Open".
Each option names what it does: run a hand with the owner's approval, accept or decline an upgrade, open a room or an outside link, mark done, snooze ("Later" hides it 3 days), or "No" (closed; not proposed again for 30 days). A decision never runs anything the red lines forbid; the owner's approval replaces the council's vote only for that one action and never lifts a cap.

As built (api/_decisions.js):
- **The store.** The open list is one JSON value written only by a compare and set on `nsoul:decisions:ver` (four tries), so a cycle raising its needs and the owner pressing a button never lose each other. Decided, resolved, expired and overflowed cards go to `nsoul:decisions:archive` (200 kept, read back 30 days); a No is remembered by key in `nsoul:decisions:no` (30 days). An open card expires on its own date (14 days unless its producer gives one). At 12 open, the oldest gives way to the newest, archived as overflow, and its producer raises it again while it is still true. A card decided by the owner is not raised again for 7 days while it says the same thing (its `stamp`: a count, a level); a changed stamp raises it at once.
- **Each producer syncs its whole set**: a card it no longer raises closes itself as resolved (the inbox read, the token renewed, Telegram linked), and a card whose reader failed this morning is kept, never closed by a fault.
- **The cycle's report** (source "cycle"): `tg-link` (only when Telegram's status is known and unlinked), `openrouter-credit`, `gsc` (raised once, 60 days), `drift:<goal>` (choose: Keep the goal, Change the goal, which is its open to the goals, Later), `verdict:<test>:<start>` (choose: Make it a lesson, which runs lesson-propose, See the test, Later, No), and `need:<its words>` (you: Done, Later) for any other line of needsYou. needsYou itself is unchanged: the chronicle and Telegram still carry it.
- **The Steward** (source "steward"): `inbox` and `journal` (you, open to #readers), `token-ig` and `token-fb` (you, open to the token clock in #posts, with the renewal steps), `token-threads` (#system), `insights-permission` (#readers).
- **The conversation** (source "conversation", key `proposal:<id>`): approve. A concrete line-up change runs lineup-skip or lineup-swap on Yes; a registered test runs experiment-plan; refresh-insights and reconcile-teach run their hands; anything vaguer is recorded (its Yes changes nothing by itself, and its steps say where to do it). Answered either on the Home or through the conversation's own Approve and Decline doors; either way the proposal leaves `nlan:proposals`.
- **Upgrades** (source "upgrade", keyed by the title's words, so a re-proposal is one card): build. Build it accepts it, No declines it; moved in the engine room instead, its card closes. A title already accepted, building or shipped is not asked about again.
- **Ideas the owner said Go to** whose doer is him: a you card with the idea's steps.
- **Deciding** (`decide(id, option)`): a hand option claims `nsoul:once:decide:<id>` (10 minutes, so two taps never run it twice) and runs through runHand with {owner:true, source:"decision", id}. Done, the card is archived "yes" with the action's id; a red line or an unknown hand archives it "refused"; any other refusal (a cap, pause) keeps it open and says why. Every answer is audited (kind "decision").
- **After the review of money (6 October 2026).** A card past its own date is never carried out: `decide` answers 410, archives it as expired and runs nothing. The No memory is written by a compare and set on `nsoul:decisions:no:ver`, so two No answers at the same moment are both remembered. The producers whose card is raised once (the sustain goal's, the month's zakat) raise it again when the cap of twelve pushed it out before he answered (archived as overflow), and never once he answered it.

## 4. Owner approval

The owner is the decision maker. runHand accepts approval {owner: true, decision or next id}: the council is not required, but the red-line check, pause, the caps and the audit still apply exactly as for the Lantern's own actions, and the audit records that the owner approved it.

As built (api/_hands.js `ownerApproval`, `OWNER_SOURCES`): the approval is read strictly, `{owner: true, source: "decision"|"next"|"idea", id}`, and anything else is no approval at all (an R2 hand then needs the council). It skips only the council. A red line is refused and the refusal audited with "the owner approved it; the red lines hold for him too"; pause refuses; the caps are reserved exactly as for the Lantern (r2 6 a day, lineup 3, experiment 1, rota 1, fix 6; an ownCapOnly lever spends only its own); the audit's "begins" and "done" entries end "approved by the owner (<source> <id>)", and the action carries `approvedBy: "owner"` and `approval`. A cycle's own R2 actions carry `approvedBy: "council"`.

A line-up change with his approval is his, as an approved Lantern proposal always was: written as `lantern-approved` with the note "Approved by the owner: ...", on any date the line-up's own validator accepts, over a slot he set before, and outside the Lantern's own per-date limits (1 skip, 2 changes), which bind only when the Lantern acts alone. Its undo puts back exactly what it replaced, while the slot still holds what it wrote.

In the cycle: a step queued from an idea he said Go to carries his approval, so the council stage records it as approved by the owner and the act stage runs it with that approval. Do it now and Skip on a step of a running cycle take the act stage's own claim (`nsoul:done:<cycle>:act:<n>`) and write `nsoul:ownerrun:<cycle>:<n>`, which the cycle reads instead of running it, so the two can never both run one step.

After the review of money (6 October 2026): only a decision he answered and a step he ran from Next are his own concrete change. A Go on an idea approves the steps the Home showed him (`ideas[].steps`, section 10) in place of the council, and nothing more: those steps keep the Lantern's own per-date limits (1 skip, 2 changes), its today-or-tomorrow rule, and never overwrite a slot he set himself; a line-up change from an idea is written as the Lantern's own ("soul") with the note "The owner's Go on an idea: ...". Do it now and Skip on a queued step claim it in the queue itself (`takenBy`, by the same compare and set the cycle's take uses, only while no cycle has it), so the cycle planning at that moment cannot take it too.

## 5. One entity

- **One record**: every action, from the cycle, the conversation or an owner button, runs through api/_hands.js runHand and lands in the same action ledger and audit chain. The conversation's old autonomous actions (refresh-insights, reconcile-teach) and its proposals (lineup-change, experiment-plan) are bridged: autonomous ones become hands with the council; proposals become decisions.
- **One memory**: the conversation reads the Lantern's state (the brief, goals with trajectories, open decisions, recent actions and their measured effects, the playbook) as a tool, so an answer in Ask knows what the cycle did this morning; and an order given in Ask ("skip tomorrow's 8 o'clock reel", "lean the afternoons toward verses for a week") becomes an intent that goes through the same hands, council and caps, and appears in Done.
- **One voice**: the Steward's operational findings feed the brief and the cycle's evidence; the ones the Lantern can repair itself (a network that failed a slot, a reel still processing) are repaired through the fix-posting lever, and a slot owed and not yet sent is left to the hourly run (6 October 2026); the ones only the owner can act on become decisions. The old Today card is replaced by Home.
- **The brief** is written at the end of each daily cycle: facts first (deterministic), then one paragraph from a model held to those facts (every number in it must be in the facts, else the template is used), stored as nsoul:brief:<date>; if no cycle ran today, Home shows the latest brief with its date.
- **Next** is a queue nsoul:queue of intents the plan dated for later (tomorrow, this week); the next cycle reviews them again before running.
- **Ideas** are written by the weekly reflection (at most 5 open), each classified by who acts. "Go" on a Lantern idea turns it into intents for the next cycle with the owner's approval recorded; "Go" on a build idea accepts the upgrade (Claude's build queue); "Go" on an owner idea becomes a "you" decision with steps.

As built:
- **One record.** The conversation acts through `lanternAct` (api/lantern-agent.js): the red lines first, then for an R2 hand the council of three over that run's own tool data (45 seconds at most), then runHand as actor "lantern". Its refresh-insights and reconcile-teach still also count against its own 5 a day. GET ?action=ledger reads the one ledger (`nsoul:action:*`) merged with the entries it kept before (`nlan:actions`), newest first; each unified item carries `unified: true`, `hand` and `title`, and its Undo, through either door, runs the hand's own recipe.
- **Orders.** The conversation's planner is handed every registered hand but the reads (`handsFor`); an action step naming one becomes an intent through `lanternAct`, and the answer ends with what the hands did, in the house's words ("Done, with the council's approval: ...", or "Not done (...): the reason"). A proposal it makes is also a decision; its SSE `proposal` event carries the card's id as `decision`.
- **One memory.** The conversation's `lantern` tool reads `lanternState` (api/_home.js): the Home without the spend, compact, totals only, with the last measured effects and the newest playbook lessons. Its planner reaches for it on questions about the Lantern's own work (what it did, plans, the brief, goals, decisions, ideas).
- **One voice.** The sense stage folds the Steward in (`stewardFold`, 15 seconds): its deterministic gather and rules (never its paragraph, never Stripe), the inbox's new count (`nb:unread`) and the journal replies waiting (`nj:queue` marks still pending in `nj:c:<entry>`). The findings are kept as `rec.steward` and given to the planner and the council as `evidence.steward`; the owner-only ones become decisions at the report. The planner is told that a network that failed a slot is asked again through a posting lever when the registry has one, and that a slot owed and not yet sent is the hourly run's, never a hand's (6 October 2026); no repair is seeded in code here (fix-posting is section 6's). api/house.js and api/_steward.js are unchanged and still answer the console.
- **The brief** (api/_home.js `writeBrief`, at the report stage, after the decisions): the facts are the north star this week and the week before (the snapshot series), site visitors and the watched share (in percent) likewise, posts sent of due yesterday (the slot records, by the snapshot's own rule), the goals met, on track and behind, what the cycle did (its R1 and R2 steps done), what it plans (the queue's first three) and the open decisions; a step that carries a model's own words (a note, a lesson, an upgrade) is said by its kind alone, so nothing rides into the brief on a note. One paragraph is asked of the free "strong" tier (30 seconds, only when it fits the tick) and kept only when it passes the guard: 3 to 5 sentences, at most 900 characters, every number (in digits or in words) one of the facts' own, no dash, no "soul", no internal id, no markup, no thinking aloud; then the sentinel reads it. Otherwise the template speaks, written from the facts alone. Kept as `nsoul:brief:<date>` with `nsoul:brief:last`, 60 days; the stored record also keeps `by` (model or template), `why` and the facts. With no cycle today, the Home shows the latest brief and its date.
- **Next.** An intent carries `when`: today (the default), tomorrow, or this week (two days on, never past Sunday); a date is kept within 7 days. A read is never queued. `nsoul:queue` holds 10 untaken at most, each for 7 days; each cycle takes at most 5 due items before it adds its own later ones, and they meet the council again unless he already approved them. Do it now runs a queued step or a cycle's unrun step at once with his approval (a queued one is claimed in the queue itself, `takenBy`, since 6 October 2026; section 4). Skip drops it and writes `nsoul:skips` for 7 days: the planner is told, and a re-proposal with the same hand and arguments is dropped in code.
- **Ideas.** The weekly reflection proposes up to 3 a week; each is checked (who acts, no red line, a Lantern idea's intents only registered, non-read hands, 3 at most) and none the owner said Never to or already holds is kept; 5 open at most (`nsoul:ideas`, compare and set). Go: a Lantern idea with intents queues them with his approval; one without becomes a directive (`nsoul:directives`) the next plan is asked once to turn into steps, which then meet the council; a build idea writes and accepts the upgrade; his own becomes a you card. Not now keeps it as later; Never writes `nsoul:ideas:never`, given to the planner and the reflection. Decided ideas show on the Home for 7 days and are kept 30.
- **Wording.** Owner-facing text passes through `sayLantern` (api/_soul.js), which changes only the house's own name for itself: the turns of phrase its code writes about itself ("the soul never overwrites it", "the soul's own goal", "a soul skip", "NOOR Soul", "the Soul room") and the capitalised Soul. The word in its meaning in the faith is never touched ("Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can carry", the /soul page). The strategist's and the reflector's prompts call the entity the Lantern and keep the word soul for its meaning in the faith.

## 6. The new levers (api/_levers.js)

- **rota-lean** (R2, cap rota 1 a day): for one reel slot (reelA to reelF), for 1 to 7 days starting today or tomorrow, the slot's kind becomes the named kind (verse, word, name, know, light, short, dua). Limits: at most 2 leans active at once; never more than 4 of the 6 reel slots the same kind on any day; the kind must have at least 30 unposted cards on the shelf; a lean ends on its own date. Read on the posting path fail-open (any fault: no lean); every view of the day (plan, today, Posts, the Lantern's line-up tool, the insights reconstruction) applies the same lean. Undo removes the lean.
- **fix-posting** (R2, cap fix 6 a day, ownCapOnly): repair what the schedule already sent today: retry a channel that failed for a slot, or finish reels still processing, using exactly the functions the Steward's buttons call. It never sends a slot (review of 6 October 2026): a slot owed and not yet sent goes out with the hourly run, one a run and the newest first, on the run's own full clock, or by the owner's Post now; `what: "send"` (or `send-slot`) is refused with a plain message. A retry holds the poster's own claim on that one network of that slot (`nsoc:retry:<date>#<slot>|<channel>`, five minutes, let go when it ends), so the hourly healer, the console's Retry and the lever never ask one network twice at once. Never a channel that already has the post (the duplicate guard decides), never anything off the schedule. No undo (a post is never deleted): the result says so.

## 7. The living Home (owner's words, 3 October 2026)

"The new Home Screen in the console needs to be visually stunning and not be only a beautiful thing to look at but a visual interactive and animated experience through which I will run things as the CEO."

The Home of sections 1 and 2 stays (same parts, same order of importance, same contract); this section is how it looks, moves and answers his hand. The idea is one picture: **the Lantern at the centre, its goals as rings of light around it**, and the day moving across a horizon beneath. Everything he can act on is reachable from that picture.

1. **The Lantern and its rings (the first thing on the screen).** A drawing (SVG, or canvas for the ambient light) about 300 px square on a phone and about 380 px on a desk. In the centre, a soft light (a lantern's glow, drawn, not a photo, no face, no figure). Around it, one ring per goal, in the goals' own order, outermost first: a faint track, a solid arc to where the goal stands now, a lighter arc on to where its projection lands by its due date, and a small mark at the target. Each goal has its own colour, in a fixed order from the palette of Islamic tilework and Mamluk enamel (lapis, emerald, pearl, saffron, turquoise, pomegranate; olive and terracotta to spare; never an alarm red, which belongs to behind and failed), checked for colour blindness on the dark ground (the dataviz validator), and never colour alone: the goals' list under the drawing (the only goals list on Home) names each ring with its status chip (met, on track, behind, no data), its numbers and a thin bar in its colour. Tapping a ring or its legend row opens that goal (a sheet on the phone, a panel on the desk): its line over time (the history, the projection, the target, today marked), what the Lantern is doing about it (`note`, `focus`), and the decisions, steps and actions that serve it.
   - **State shows in the light.** Working: the light breathes slowly (about 6 seconds a breath). Needs you: a golden ripple leaves the light every few seconds and the status chip itself becomes the gold "2 need you" button; tapping it scrolls to the decisions. Paused: the light sinks to an ember and the rings go quiet and grey.
   - **Arrival.** The rings draw from nothing to their values (under a second, one after another), the numbers count up. Later reads move a ring from its old value to its new one.
   - **Ornament.** Behind the drawing, a very faint lattice of the eight fold star of Islamic geometry, turning very slowly. Only eight fold (or ten or twelve fold) Islamic geometry: never a six pointed star, never a cross, never any symbol of another faith, never a face or a figure.
2. **The day across the horizon.** Under the drawing, a line for today with every posting slot at its hour (`today.slots`, section 10): sent is a lit point, partly sent half lit, failed an ember that flickers gently, still to come a hollow point, skipped struck through. A thin "now" mark moves along the line with the clock. Tapping a point says what it is ("19:00, a verse reel, sent to 5 of 6 networks") and links to Posts.
3. **The brief** appears sentence by sentence; its numbers count up; each number carries a small line of its last 14 days (`brief.numbers[].series`).
4. **Decisions are the desk.** Each card carries its goal's colour (a dot, and on a desk hovering or focusing the card makes that goal's ring glow). Answering: the pressed button fills, the card folds away, a small light travels to Done and the next card rises; a toast says what happened, with Undo when the answer carries an action that has one. A card with a draft (section 8) shows "Read the draft" and "Copy". Confirms stay inside the card.
5. **Next is a timeline** (today, tomorrow, this week, the calendar) on a thin line of light. "Do it now" turns into a working state, then the step slides into Done. A blocked step shows its reason; when `canDoNow` is true it offers "Do it anyway", which asks inside the card with the reason quoted (his approval may run what the council refused; the red lines and caps still hold). When `canDoNow` is false there is no button.
6. **Done is what moved**, newest first, each with its goal's dot; what arrived since his last visit glows once.
7. **Ideas** show their expected effect; Go lights the card and sends it on (a step, a card or a build) with a short line saying where it went; Not now slides it aside; Never lets it fade.
8. **Keeping the lamp lit** (section 9, the `giving` part): the lamp of Ayat an-Nur (24:35), a Mamluk glass mosque lamp hanging in a pointed niche, only geometry and plain bands on it, whose light rises within the glass as the month's gifts cover the house's running costs (full when covered, and then the words say "already cover" with no percent; when the costs are not recorded, the light follows the monthly givers against their goal instead and says so), with the monthly givers and their change, the month's gifts and the last 30 days in the house's currency, the zakat waiting to be given if any, and the last thing the Lantern did for giving. A link to the ledger room. Never a name, an email, a du'a or a single gift.
9. **Alive.** Home reads itself again every 90 seconds while it is open and visible, and at once after any of his actions; differences animate in place; a quiet "updated 12:04" says when. Nothing polls while the tab is hidden.
10. **Motion rules.** Feedback 150 to 300 ms, arrivals and changes 400 to 900 ms, ambient loops 4 to 8 seconds. Only transforms and opacity, the Web Animations API and requestAnimationFrame, the loops stopped while hidden. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` there is no ambient loop, no count, no travel: every change simply appears. No library and no CDN.
11. **Layout.** Phone first (390 px): the drawing with the status and Pause, the needs-you chip and the horizon on the first screen; the goals' list follows the drawing; then the brief, the desk, Next, Done, the lamp, Ideas, Ask, the engine room door. On a desk (1440 px), a cockpit: the drawing, the horizon, the goals' list and the lamp on one side; the brief, the desk and Done in the middle; Next, Ideas and Ask on the other. Tap targets at least 44 px, no sideways scroll, every drawing with its words beside it for a screen reader.

## 8. The Lantern's mission powers (owner's words, 3 October 2026)

"More power to act has to encompass everything that will help reach the goal: Serve Allah in the most perfect way, shape or form possible and spread Islam to everyone in the world with peace, not only having power over the reels, but actively working towards the goals taking concrete actions."

The Lantern already reads everything and acts on the posting (line-up, rota-lean, fix-posting, experiments), the numbers (insights-refresh, reconcile-teach) and search (indexnow-submit). These powers reach the rest of what the house controls. ### 16.1 The day's letters in one line of the log (8 October 2026)

The console sits behind the owner's key, so the house's director reads the morning's letters from the log.
