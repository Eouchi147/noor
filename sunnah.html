A word of the Path

Sunnah السُّنَّة

Editorial

The Prophet's way in belief, worship and conduct, and the measure against which innovation is judged.

What does Sunnah mean in Islam?

The word means a trodden path. In hadith study it covers what the Prophet peace be upon him said, did, and quietly approved; in fiqh it also names a recommended act; in creed it means the received way of the first generations, which is why the phrase Ahl al-Sunnah exists. Set against bid'ah, sunnah means the shape of the religion as it was handed over, rather than anyone's personal preference.
