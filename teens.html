For Teenagers

You are not a problem to be managed. This room talks to you the way the tradition talked to the young it trusted with everything: straight, with evidence, and without a lecture.

One

You are not an afterthought

الثِّقَة

ath-thiqa · The generation that carried Islam first was, to a startling degree, your age.

Sunnah

The young around him ﷺ

Ali accepted Islam as a boy. Mus'ab ibn Umayr was sent, young, as the first teacher of an entire city, and Madinah opened for Islam through him. Usama ibn Zayd was given command of an army with elder companions in its ranks while still in his teens. Aisha's scholarship, begun young, taught the ummah for decades.

The Prophet ﷺ did not park the young at the edge of the room until they were forty. He gave them the centre of it. Whatever your masjid does with its teenagers, this is what he did with his.

the Seerah
Sunnah

A youth grown in worship

Among the seven shaded by Allah on the Day when there is no shade but His: a young person who grew up in the worship of their Lord (Bukhari and Muslim). Not an elder who arrived at wisdom, a youth, precisely because the pull of these years is fiercer and the choice worth more.

The hardest years to hold the rope are the most rewarded ones for holding it. That is not a consolation prize; it is the actual exchange rate.

Bukhari & Muslim

Two

Doubts & questions

السُّؤَال

as-su'al · A doubt is not a sin. It is a question that has not found its scholar yet.

Qur’an

Questions built this house

Ask the people of knowledge if you do not know (16:43). The companions asked the Prophet ﷺ relentlessly, about the soul, the Hour, the moon, wine, orphans, and the Qur'an answers questions by name: they ask you about... is one of its refrains.

So ask. A faith that survives only when nobody questions it would not be worth your one life. This one has been answering hard questions for fourteen centuries, in writing.

Qur’an 16:43
Editorial

How to search honestly

The internet will hand you a furious clip for every position, attackers and defenders alike, and none of them will footnote anything. Honest searching looks different: whole books over clips, named scholars over anonymous accounts, and both sides of a claim before a verdict.

This site was built for exactly that: the encyclopedia for every term, the evidence badges on every claim, the Lantern for the question you are embarrassed to ask out loud. Nobody here logs who asked what; ask freely.

Three

Friends & the crowd

الصُّحْبَة

as-suhba · Nobody drifts alone. People drift in convoys.

Sunnah

You become your circle

A person is upon the way of his close friend, so let each of you look well at whom he befriends (Tirmidhi 2378). Not the crowd, the close friend: the two or three whose habits quietly become yours.

Audit the inner ring once in a while. Do they make salah easier or embarrassing? Do you leave them better or smaller? You do not need to burn bridges; you need to choose which two or three hands you hold.

Tirmidhi 2378
Editorial

The one who doesn't

There is a way to be the one who doesn't, doesn't drink, doesn't mock, doesn't go where the night is going, without a sermon and without superiority. A plain no thanks, delivered relaxed, repeated identically, is respected within a month and famous within a year.

The friends worth keeping are the ones who test you once and then guard your no for you. The ones who test you nightly were never company; they were recruiters.

  • A person is on the way of his close friend, so look well at whom you befriend (Tirmidhi 2378).
  • The outer rings shout, yet only the inner ring shapes you. Keep it small and keep it chosen.

The rings. The solid circle is the only one you fully control.

Four

The scroll & the screen

الشَّاشَة

ash-shasha · The feed is not evil. It is engineered, which is different, and you can out-engineer it.

Editorial

Built to hold you

Endless feeds run on variable reward, the same mechanism as a slot machine: mostly nothing, sometimes gold, so the thumb keeps pulling. Knowing this does not free you by itself, but it changes the question from what is wrong with me to how is this built.

The counter-engineering is boring and it works: the phone charges outside the bedroom, prayer times break the scroll five times a day by design, and one app deleted outright beats ten limits negotiated nightly.

Editorial

Make, don't only scroll

An hour consumed leaves nothing; an hour made, code, a drawing, a rep count, a memorised page, compounds. The early scholars of this deen were teenagers who made things: they memorised, copied, travelled and taught while their peers watched the seventh-century equivalent of anything.

One honest test of any app: after an hour on it, do you have anything? Keep the tools that leave a residue. Starve the ones that don't.

Five

Your body, an amanah

الأَمَانَة

al-amana · Desire, sleep and strength are not enemies of the deen. They are equipment, on loan.

Sunnah

Desire has a lane

The Prophet ﷺ addressed young men directly: whoever among you is able, let him marry; and whoever is not, let him fast, for it is a shield (Bukhari 5065). Notice what is missing from that sentence: shame. Desire is treated as real, normal, and in need of a lane, not a curse to pretend you don't have.

The lane is marriage, and until then, the shield: fasting, training hard, and keeping the triggers out of arm's reach. That is the whole programme, and it is fourteen centuries old.

Bukhari 5065
Editorial

The trap on every screen

Pornography deserves plain words: it is engineered like the feed but aimed at the strongest drive you have, it escalates by design, and it quietly rewrites what you expect from real people. Millions are stuck in it; you are not uniquely broken if you have struggled.

The way out is also plain: filters you do not hold the keys to, the phone out of the bedroom, fajr as the anchor that makes the night worth protecting, one trusted person told, and patience with relapse, repentance is re-aiming, not self-hatred. The Protection and Health rooms carry more.

Editorial

Sleep and strength

Sleep is one of the largest levers on mood, grades and self-control there is, and the phone is one of its biggest thieves. Guard the last hour of the night and the body follows.

Train something. The Prophet ﷺ raced, wrestled and praised the strong believer; a body that lifts, runs or grapples burns the restlessness that otherwise ferments into worse. The Health room has the whole map.

Muslim 2664

Six

Hard days at home

الوَالِدَان

al-walidan · The Qur'an knows parents are not always easy. Its instruction survives that knowledge.

Qur’an

Even 'uff' is too much

Your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and be excellent to parents. If one or both reach old age with you, do not say to them even 'uff', and do not repel them, and speak to them a noble word (17:23). The bar is that high, and it was set by the One who knows exactly how trying a household can be.

Respect is not agreement. You can hold your own view, even be right, and still keep your voice noble. The muscle you build doing that is the same one every good marriage and every good leadership runs on later.

Qur’an 17:23-24
Editorial

When home is genuinely hard

Some homes are heavier than a khutbah admits: anger, control, or worse. The Qur'an itself imagines parents pressing a child towards wrong and still says: accompany them in this world with honour, and do not obey the wrong (31:15). Obedience has limits; kindness does not require surrender.

If home hurts in ways you cannot carry, telling a trusted adult, an imam, a relative, a school counsellor, is not betrayal. It is the sunnah of seeking help, and you are allowed to use it.

Qur’an 31:15

Seven

A path of your own

الطَّرِيق

at-tariq · Not your parents' deen by inertia. Yours, chosen, small and unbreakable.

Sunnah

Small and constant

The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most constant, even if small (Bukhari 6464). Five prayers, kept, outweigh a Ramadan of fireworks followed by eleven months of nothing. Build the floor, not the highlight reel.

Pick one thing this month: the five on time, or one page of Qur'an a day, or fajr specifically. One. When it has held for thirty days, add the next. That is how every unshakeable Muslim you admire was actually built.

Bukhari 6464
Editorial

When it gets dark

Sadness and anger are not weak iman; prophets grieved and said so. When it closes in, you have the du'a of Yunus from inside the whale, la ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimin (21:87), and you have the verse that outranks every despair: do not despair of the mercy of Allah; He forgives all sins (39:53).

And you have people. Talking to someone, a friend, an imam, a doctor, is deen, not the failure of it. The Prophet ﷺ was consoled by Khadijah on the hardest day of his life; being helped is sunnah.

Qur’an 21:87Qur’an 39:53
  • Whatever the day throws, five fixed points return you to yourself and to your Lord.
  • Small and steady outweighs grand and abandoned: the most beloved deeds are the most constant (Bukhari 6464).

The anchored day. Five points that do not move, under a line that does.
