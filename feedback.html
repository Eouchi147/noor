Corrections and Ideas

التَّصْحِيح

Tell us what we got wrong

A correction is a gift, and we would rather be corrected than admired. Send a mistake, a broken page, or an idea, and it reaches the people who keep this house.

Every correction is a gift

This Codex was built by hand, and a hand can slip. If you find a verse cited wrongly, a hadith graded too kindly, a translation that misses, a date out of place, or a button that does nothing, telling us is a service to everyone who reads after you.

Nothing here is written from a pedestal. If you know better, say so plainly. If you are unsure, say that too, and we will look.

مَا نَوْعُهُ

What would you like to send?

Choose one, and the note below shapes itself to help you say the useful thing quickly.

Pick a kind above and this line will tell you what helps most.

Leave it empty and your note stays anonymous. Nothing is tracked and nothing is stored on this page beyond your own device.

Or, if you would rather write from your own inbox: open my mail app with this note ready.

Copied. Paste it into an email to hello@noorcodex.com and it will reach us.

Where it goes. Straight onto the desk of the small team that keeps this house, and nowhere else. You may also write to hello@noorcodex.com if you prefer; it is the same desk. There is no ticket system and no queue of departments. Corrections about a verse or a hadith are checked against the sources before anything changes, and when we are wrong we fix it and say so.

الرِّسَالَة

Writing to us about a masjid, a school, or an organisation

Different door, same address.

1If you want to put a room of the Codex inside your own website or app, everything you need is on the For Schools and Organizations page, including the embed code, which is free to take. If your teaching is free, your use is free.

2If you run a masjid, the Masjid Toolbox holds the prayer board, the khutba builder, the printable timetable and the qibla tool, all free and needing no account.

3For anything else, write to hello@noorcodex.com and say what you need in one paragraph.

الأَمَانَة

What we promise

We read everything. Not every note gets a reply, but every note is read.

Corrections come first. A mistake about the words of Allah or the words of His Messenger ﷺ is not a small thing, and it moves to the front of the queue.

We say when we were wrong. Where a claim was graded too generously or a reference was wrong, the page is fixed and the fix is honest, not quiet.

Your note is not a product. There is no mailing list here, no advertising, and nothing is sold. Your words are used to improve the Codex and for nothing else.
