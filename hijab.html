A word of the Path

Hijab حِجاب

Editorial

The covering a Muslim woman wears in public, most often a headscarf with modest clothing.

What does Hijab mean in Islam?

The command to draw the khimar over the neckline stands in Surah al-Nur, directly after the command to believing men to lower their gaze; the outer garment, the jilbab, is in Surah al-Ahzab. The word itself means a screen or curtain, and in the Qur'an it usually means a barrier rather than a garment; the everyday sense of headscarf is later usage. The schools agree on covering the hair and body and differ over the face and hands.
