٢ · Privacy

What we know about you: almost nothing

No trackers, no ad cookies, no profiles. The Codex does not run advertising trackers, does not set cookies for readers, does not fingerprint you, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone. This is a promise of the house, not a legal minimum.

Region detection happens on your device. To show at most one local Guardian line, your browser checks its own timezone and language settings locally. That check never leaves your device. The page then asks our server only "which lamps are lit this week", a question that contains nothing about you.

Aggregate counts. The Codex keeps its own cookie-free lamp counter: each visit adds one to a daily total, a country total (from the country code our host attaches to requests) and a room total. No IP address, no identifier, no fingerprint is ever stored, and nothing can be traced back to you. It counts lights, not people’s lives.

The Lantern. Questions you type to the Lantern are sent to our server and passed to an AI provider once, to generate the answer, then the conversation lives only in your own browser tab and is gone when you close it. We do not build profiles from questions. Please do not type personal identifying details into the Lantern; it does not need them to help you.

Payments. Guardian payments are processed entirely by Stripe. Card numbers never touch our servers. We see what Stripe shows any merchant: the sponsor's name, email, chosen region and payment status, and we use it only to run the sponsorship.

Email. If you write to us, we use your address to reply. Nothing else.

For any privacy question or request (including deletion of anything you believe we hold), write to hello@noorcodex.com.
