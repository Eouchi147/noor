٢ · Privacy

What we know about you: almost nothing

No trackers, no ad cookies, no profiles. The Codex does not run advertising trackers, does not set cookies for readers, does not fingerprint you, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone. This is a promise of the house, not a legal minimum.

Aggregate counts. The Codex keeps its own cookie-free lamp counter: each visit adds one to a daily total, a country total (from the country code our host attaches to requests) and a room total. No IP address, no identifier, no fingerprint is ever stored, and nothing can be traced back to you. It counts lights, not people’s lives.

The Lantern. Questions you type to the Lantern are sent to our server and passed to an AI provider once, to generate the answer, then the conversation lives only in your own browser tab and is gone when you close it. We do not build profiles from questions. Please do not type personal identifying details into the Lantern; it does not need them to help you.

Payments. All payments are processed entirely by Stripe. Card numbers never touch our servers; we see only what Stripe shows any merchant (name, email, amount, status) and use it solely to run the gift or license.

Email. If you write to us, we use your address to reply. Nothing else.

For any privacy question or request (including deletion of anything you believe we hold), write to hello@noorcodex.com.

٤ · Gifts

Gifts, plainly

The Codex accepts voluntary gifts (sadaqa) from readers to keep the library free, ad-free and tracker-free. A gift is support of the work; it buys no goods, no services, no influence over content, and no recognition unless we choose to give it.

  • Not zakat, and we do not ask for yours. A gift here is sadaqa, voluntary charity. NOOR is not a registered charity and issues no tax receipt, so nothing given here should be counted as your zakat. Zakat is its own obligation: 2.5% of the wealth you have held for a lunar year, owed to the eight categories named in the Qur'an (9:60). Pay it wherever you judge it will do the most good. The Codex teaches the obligation because it is an obligation, not because it wants the money.
  • A gift is unrestricted. It is voluntary support for the work and for the keeper's charitable intentions. It is not earmarked, it buys no goods or services, and it creates no legal trust, no agency and no obligation to spend it in any particular way. If you need your giving directed to a named project with published accounts, give to a registered charity that offers that; this is not that.
  • The one figure that is promised. As a standing commitment of the keeper, 2.5% of money received is set aside and given onward to zakat-eligible causes, the categories of Qur'an 9:60. That share does not change.
  • Masajid, on trust. Beyond that share, part of what comes in is meant for helping raise masajid where they are needed. No percentage is stated, no project is named, no timetable is given and no accounting is published. It is an intention held before Allah, not a contract with the giver, and it is offered as trust in the one who carries this Codex rather than as a promise you may enforce. Give only if you are content with that.
  • The rising light. The illustration on the giving page is exactly that and nothing more: a horizon where small lights gather. It is not a fundraising thermometer, it shows no amount, and it reports no total, because none is published.
  • Visitor counts. The Codex counts visits in aggregate, cookie-free, so the keeper can understand how many people this work reaches: the day, the country, the room, and a coarse source such as reddit or google. It never builds advertising profiles, never sells or shares personal information, and never follows anyone around the web. Payment details exist only inside Stripe when someone chooses to give or license.
  • One-time or monthly. Monthly gifts renew through Stripe until cancelled; cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt email or by writing to us, effective for all future months.
  • Honest mistakes are refunded. A wrong amount, a duplicate, a child with a card: write within 7 days to hello@noorcodex.com and it comes back in full. Otherwise gifts are final.
  • Du’as left at checkout may be shown on the site as a small anonymous line so the community can pray with you: the du’a text only, never your name, email or amount, and only after review for suitability. Leave the field empty for a fully private gift, and write to us any time to have a du’a taken down.
  • Anonymity is the rule. Givers are never named or listed anywhere on the site, and we never publish, sell or share giver details. What Stripe needs to process a payment (card, email for the receipt) stays inside Stripe; your gift remains between you and Allah.
  • Gifts are processed by Stripe in your currency where supported; card details never touch our servers.
