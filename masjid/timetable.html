The printable monthly timetable

A full month of prayer times computed from the sun itself, with sunrise, optional iqama columns and hijri dates, on one clean sheet made to be printed and pinned to a wall. No screen, no power, no account.

1Your place

On a map, a right click on your building gives latitude then longitude. South is a negative latitude, west is a negative longitude.

3Iqama minutes after adhan

Leave a box empty and that prayer gets no iqama column. Leave them all empty and the sheet prints adhan times alone.

4The sheet

Print gives one month on one page. Choose portrait, A4 or Letter, and leave headers and footers off in the print dialog for the cleanest sheet.
