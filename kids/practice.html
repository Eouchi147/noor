١ The Big Sentence

The whole religion fits in one breath

Every Muslim in the world — every grandmother in Makkah, every kid in Canada — agrees on one sentence. It's the first thing whispered to babies and the password of the whole ummah:

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

La ilaha illa Allah, Muhammadun rasul Allah

There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.

That's it. That's the key to the whole house. If you can say it and mean it, you're in the family.

٢ Wudu — Getting Sparkly

Before we pray, we wash — like getting ready to meet Someone important. Because we are.

🤲Handswash them×3
👄Mouthswish water×3
👃Nosesniff & blow×3
😊Faceforehead to chin×3
💪Armsto the elbows×3
💆Head & earswipe once×1
🦶Feetup to the ankles — and you're sparkly clean×3

Start with Bismillah, and remember: wudu isn't a race. Water is precious — the Prophet ﷺ used only a little, even beside a river.

٤ Little Golden Words

Tiny sentences that turn ordinary moments into treasure

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismillah

"In Allah's name" — before eating, drinking, or starting anything

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

Alhamdulillah

"All thanks belong to Allah" — after eating, after sneezing, after anything good

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya

"In Your name, O Allah, I sleep and I wake" — the goodnight du'a (Bukhari 6324)

٥ The Secret Level: Kindness

Worship isn't only on the prayer mat

The Prophet ﷺ taught that smiling at someone is charity (Tirmidhi 1956), that Allah loves gentle hands, and that the best Muslims are the best to their families. So:

🌟 Say salam when you come home · 🌟 Help without being asked twice · 🌟 Don't waste water or food · 🌟 Be soft with animals · 🌟 Tell the truth even when it's squeaky-hard

Every one of these counts on your scale. That's the secret level most grown-ups forget.

For parents · the minimum & the reasonable maximum

The floor: children are invited to pray from around seven and expected to keep the habit from around ten (Abu Dawud 495 · hasan) — but accountability before Allah begins only at maturity. Before then, everything is practice, and practice is meant to be praised, not policed.

The reasonable ceiling: a child who loves five short prayers will outgrow a child who was forced through fifty. Add Qur'an in verses, fasting in half-days, and let them see you loving it — the sunnah of teaching is patience: "Allah is gentle and loves gentleness in all things" (Bukhari 6927).
