The Big Sentence

The whole religion fits in one breath

Every Muslim in the world, every grandmother in Makkah, every kid in Canada, agrees on one sentence. It is the first thing whispered to babies and the password of the whole ummah:

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

La ilaha illa Allah, Muhammadun rasul Allah

There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.

Ask a grown-up to read it with you.

That's it. That's the key to the whole house. If you can say it and mean it, you're in the family.

Build the Big Sentence: tap the words in the right order.

لَاإِلَٰهَإِلَّااللَّهُمُحَمَّدٌرَسُولُاللَّهِ

Tap the first word!Yes! Now the next word.Not that one. Listen, then try again!You built the Big Sentence!

The key to the whole house!

Say it slowly, and mean it, and you are in the family.

Wudu, Getting Sparkly

Ask a grown-up to read it with you.

Sparkly clean!

Seven steps, and now you are ready to stand in front of Allah.

The Five Prayers and Their Skies

Each prayer has its own sky. Look at the window and you will always know which one it is.

Which prayer is it? Look at the sky, then tap the prayer.

الفَجْرal-Fajr
الظُّهْرadh-Dhuhr
العَصْرal-'Asr
المَغْرِبal-Maghrib
العِشَاءal-'Isha

Ask a grown-up to read it with you.

Five prayers, five skies, every day!

The Secret Level: Kindness

Worship is not only on the prayer mat

The Prophet ﷺ taught that smiling at someone is charity (Tirmidhi 1956), that Allah loves gentle hands, and that the best Muslims are the best to their families. Every kind thing you do is weighed on your scale.

Kind or not kind? Tap the right answer and fill your scale.

🏠Say salam when you come home
🐈Pull the cat's tail
🧹Help before being asked twice
🚰Leave the tap running while you play
😊Smile at someone
🧸Say the toy broke by itself when you broke it
🍞Finish your food instead of throwing it away
🗣️Tell the truth even when it's squeaky-hard
Your scale is heavy with good!

Every one of these counts. That is the secret level most grown-ups forget.

Sort all the cards first, then your star is ready!

For parents · the minimum & the reasonable maximum

The floor: children are invited to pray from around seven and expected to keep the habit from around ten (Abu Dawud 495 · hasan), but accountability before Allah begins only at maturity. Before then, everything is practice, and practice is meant to be praised, not policed.

The reasonable ceiling: a child who loves five short prayers will outgrow a child who was forced through fifty. Add Qur'an in verses, fasting in half-days, and let them see you loving it. The sunnah of teaching is patience: "Allah is gentle and loves gentleness in all things" (Bukhari 6927).
