Round 1
The story of Yunus

Three darknesses. Three words.

Yunus was inside the whale. The whale was inside the sea. The sea was inside the night. Dark, dark, dark.

But Yunus knew Who to call. He said three glowing words. Find them in the deep, one by one, and watch the light come back.

Tap the glowing word when it floats by. Little bubbles are just for popping.

The sea filled with light
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّالِمِينَ
La ilaha illa Anta · Subhanak · inni kuntu minaz-zalimin
"So We answered him and saved him from distress. And so do We save the believers."
(Qur'an 21:88)
