The Little Mushaf

Eleven short surahs, one ayah at a time. Read the Arabic, say it after the reciter, learn what it means, and light a star when you can say it by yourself.

Surahs you can say: 0 of 11

Start with Al-Fatiha, then Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas: the four every child learns first. Say each ayah after the reciter three times, then try it alone.
