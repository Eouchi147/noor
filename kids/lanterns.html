The Lantern Sky

Every gift to the Codex becomes a lantern here, carrying the giver's du'a with no name attached. Tap a lantern to watch it bloom, read the du'a inside, and answer it with one beautiful word: Ameen.

The sky is waiting for its first lanterns. When gifts arrive with du'as, they will float here.
