Hajar’s Search

Run between Safa and Marwah, dig for the spring, and share Zamzam.

The valley is empty and the sun is hot. Baby Ismail is thirsty, so Mother Hajar runs to look for water.

Seven times she climbed Safa and Marwah. Then Allah sent help, and water sprang up beside the baby.

Hajar made a little wall of sand and said: Zam, zam! Birds came, then a caravan, then a whole town.
