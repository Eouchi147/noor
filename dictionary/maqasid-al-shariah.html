The meaning

The meaning

What Maqasid al-Shari'ah means in Islam

Al-Ghazali and later al-Shatibi set out five: religion, life, intellect, lineage and property, with dignity and sometimes freedom added by later scholars. Interests are ranked as necessities, needs and refinements, so what preserves life outweighs what merely makes life more comfortable. Maqasid explain why a ruling exists and help reasoning where the texts are silent, but most scholars insist they cannot be used to set a clear text aside.
