خَمْر

Khamr

Intoxicants. Wine and anything else that clouds the mind, forbidden in the Qur'an.

Also written · Khamar · Hamr · Khumur · Muskir · Muskirat · Nabidh · Sharab · alcohol · wine · intoxicants · liquor · drinking · drugs · booze

The meaning

The meaning

What Khamr means in Islam

The prohibition arrived in stages, ending with the command to shun it as a defilement from the work of Satan. The Prophet's rule that whatever intoxicates in large amounts is forbidden in small amounts extends the ban to every intoxicant, and it is the textbook example of qiyas at work. Hanafis kept the word khamr technically for grape wine while forbidding other intoxicants under a separate heading, arriving at the same practical result. Scholars differ over intoxicating substances used as medicine where no alternative exists.
