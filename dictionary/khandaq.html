Khandaq

The siege of Madinah in year five, met by digging a trench across the city's open side.

Also written · Battle of the Trench · Ghazwat al-Khandaq · Al-Ahzab · Battle of Ahzab · Jang e Khandaq · Trench · Hendek · Khandak · Confederates · Battle of the Confederates · Khandaq battle

The meaning

What Khandaq means in Islam

A confederacy of Quraysh and allied tribes came to finish the matter, outnumbering the defenders several times over. Salman al-Farisi suggested the Persian tactic of a trench, which the Arabs had not seen, and the Muslims dug it in bitter cold and hunger with stones tied against their stomachs. After weeks of siege a winter wind tore the camp apart and the confederacy went home. Surah al-Ahzab is named for them and describes the fear of that month plainly.
