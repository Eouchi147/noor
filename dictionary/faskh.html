Faskh

Annulment: a judge dissolving a marriage for a defect or a failure of duty.

Also written · Fask · Faskh nikah · Fasakh · Fesih · Tafriq · Tatliq · annulment · judicial divorce · dissolution of marriage · court divorce · cancelling a marriage

The meaning

The meaning

What Faskh means in Islam

Grounds recognised across the schools include serious undisclosed illness, impotence, insanity, desertion, imprisonment, failure to maintain, and harm. Malikis are the broadest here, allowing dissolution for harm in general, which is why courts in many countries follow them on this point even where another school prevails. Faskh differs from talaq because it uses up none of the husband's pronouncements, and from khul' because it requires no payment from the wife.
