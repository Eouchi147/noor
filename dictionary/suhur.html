سُحور

Suhur

The meal eaten before dawn to begin a day of fasting.

Also written · Sehri · Sahur · Suhoor · Sohour · Sahri · Sehri time · pre dawn meal · Sahoor · Sohur

The meaning

The meaning

What Suhur means in Islam

The name comes from sahar, the last darkness before dawn. The Prophet said to eat it because there is blessing in it, and encouraged delaying it until close to the dawn. It is a sunnah rather than a condition, so a fast is still valid if a person sleeps through it.

Evidence · Sunnah · Established in the authentic Sunnah
