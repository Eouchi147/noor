What Shafi'i means in Islam

Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i died in 204 AH and wrote the Risalah, the first surviving work of usul al-fiqh. His school prevails in Egypt, East Africa, Yemen, Kurdistan and much of Southeast Asia. It leans on hadith more readily than the Hanafis do, and it rejects istihsan as a source in its own right, holding that preference without evidence is lawmaking.
