The meaning

What Al-Fitnah al-Ula means in Islam

Fitnah means a fire that tests metal, and then any trial that separates people. It ran from the thirty fifth to the fortieth year of the Hijrah: the murder of Uthman, the Battle of the Camel, Siffin, the failed arbitration, and the breaking away of the Kharijites who then killed Ali. Sunni scholarship holds that both sides were Companions acting on interpretation, that Ali was closer to the truth, and that the settled adab is to restrain the tongue about what happened.
