لَا أَصْلَ لَهُ

La asla lahu

A verdict meaning the saying has no chain at all, so it cannot even be graded.

Also written · La asl lahu · Laa asla lahu · La aslun lahu · La asla lah · No basis · No isnad · Baseless hadith · Has no basis

What La asla lahu means in Islam

This is stronger than calling something weak. A weak hadith has a chain that fails; a saying with no basis was never transmitted in the first place. Such sayings are usually folk wisdom, or a scholar's own phrase that drifted into quotation marks. A great deal of what circulates as hadith online belongs here.
