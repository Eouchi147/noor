عَامُ الفِيل

Am al-Fil

The year an army with an elephant marched on the Ka'bah and was destroyed, around 570.

Also written · Aam al-Fil · Year of the Elephant · Am ul Fil · Amul Feel · Aam ul Feel · Abraha · Abrahah · Ashab al-Fil · Year of Elephant · Fil year · Am al Feel

The meaning

The meaning

What Am al-Fil means in Islam

Abrahah, ruling Yemen, came to pull the Ka'bah down so pilgrims would come to his own church instead. Surah al-Fil describes birds pelting the army with stones of baked clay until they were left like chewed straw. The Prophet was born in that year or close to it, and Arabs dated events from it for a generation afterwards. Abd al-Muttalib's answer to Abrahah is famous: the House has a Lord who will protect it.
