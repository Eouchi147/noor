Sama'

Receiving a hadith by hearing it directly from a teacher, the strongest of the ways to take a report.

Also written · Sama · Samaa · Samaah · Sima · Sema · Hearing · Tahammul al-ilm · Sama hadith · Sama al-hadith · Munawalah · Wijadah · Qiraah

The meaning

What Sama' means in Islam

Scholars listed several ways of receiving hadith, in descending strength: hearing the teacher recite it, reading it back to him, a written licence, being handed a manuscript, correspondence, and finding a book with no licence at all. The wording of a chain tells you which way was used, so haddathana means he narrated to us in person, while akhbarana usually signals that it was read back to him.
