السَّكْتَة

Saktah

A brief silence held without taking a breath, then the recitation carries straight on.

Also written · Sakta · Sakt · Saktah Hafs · Sakta in Quran · brief pause · silent pause · Saktat · Sakta Hafs · pause without breath

The meaning

The meaning

What Saktah means in Islam

It keeps two words from running together in a way that could mislead the ear. In the Hafs reading there are four of them, in al-Kahf, Ya-Sin, al-Qiyamah and al-Mutaffifin, and the mushaf marks each with a small seen. Other readings place their pauses differently.

Evidence · Sunnah · Established in the authentic Sunnah
