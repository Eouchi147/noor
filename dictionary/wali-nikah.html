وَلِيّ

Wali (marriage guardian)

The male relative who contracts a woman's marriage on her behalf, usually her father.

Also written · Wali · Walee · Waliy · Vali · Veli · Wali nikah · Wali mujbir · Awliya · guardian · marriage guardian · bride's guardian · who gives the bride away

The meaning

The meaning

What Wali (marriage guardian) means in Islam

Malikis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis hold a marriage invalid without one, citing the report that there is no marriage without a wali. Hanafis hold that an adult woman may contract her own marriage, though her guardian may object to a plainly unsuitable match. Whether a father may marry off a virgin daughter without her word is the oldest dispute here: Hanafis say no, the other three allowed it under conditions, Ibn Taymiyyah rejected it. The Prophet annulled the marriage of a woman whose father had contracted it against her will.
