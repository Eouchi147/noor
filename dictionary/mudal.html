المُعْضَل

Mu'dal

A chain missing two or more narrators in a row, a wider break than munqati'.

Also written · Mudal · Muadal · Mudhal · Muadhal · Mu'adal · Mudal hadith · Two narrators missing · Mu'dal hadeeth

The meaning

The meaning

What Mu'dal means in Islam

The word suggests something troublesome and hard to solve. It is more serious than a single missing link, since a gap that size can hide an entire generation. When a later writer quotes the Prophet with no chain at all, the result is usually mu'dal in effect.
