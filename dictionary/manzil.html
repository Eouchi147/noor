مَنْزِل

Manzil

One of seven divisions of the Qur'an, used by those who complete a full reading each week.

Also written · Manazil · Manzeel · seven manzils · Manzil Quran · weekly division · Manzil dua · Manzil e Quran · seven parts of the Quran

The meaning

The meaning

What Manzil means in Islam

Companions are reported to have divided their reading this way, in portions of three, five, seven, nine, eleven and thirteen surahs, with the remainder of the mushaf as the seventh. It is an older habit than the thirty part division and is still followed by some. A separate booklet of protective verses is also called Manzil; it borrowed the name but is a different thing.
