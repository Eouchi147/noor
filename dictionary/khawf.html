Khawf

Fear of Allah: the awareness of His justice that keeps a person from walking off the edge.

Also written · Khauf · Khowf · Kawf · Khof · Khawf min Allah · Khashyah · Khashya · Fear of Allah · Fear of God · Godfearing · Fear of punishment

The meaning

The meaning

What Khawf means in Islam

The Qur'an describes believers as those whose hearts tremble when Allah is mentioned (Qur'an 8:2), and as calling on their Lord in fear and hope together (Qur'an 32:16). Scholars say the two wings must stay even: fear alone tips into despair, hope alone tips into carelessness. Healthy khawf is measured by what it makes you do, not by how bad it makes you feel.
