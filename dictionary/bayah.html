بَيْعة

Bay'ah

A pledge of allegiance, given to a ruler, or in Sufi orders to a spiritual guide.

Also written · Bayah · Baya · Bayat · Baiah · Bai'ah · Biat · Beyat · Bayah to a shaykh · pledge of allegiance · oath of allegiance · allegiance · homage

The meaning

The meaning

What Bay'ah means in Islam

The word comes from bay', a sale, since the one who pledges gives loyalty and receives protection in return. The Qur'an records the pledge under the tree and the pledge of the believing women, and the Prophet took bay'ah on obedience in what is good, a condition scholars stress: there is no obedience to a creature in disobedience to the Creator. In Sufi orders bay'ah to a shaykh is a commitment to a path of training, which is a different use of the same word.
