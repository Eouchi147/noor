مَذْهَب

Madhhab

A school of law: a settled method of deriving rulings, carried by generations of scholars.

Also written · Mazhab · Madhab · Mathhab · Maslak · Mezhep · Madhhabs · Madhahib · Mazahib · school of thought · school of law · Islamic school · fiqh school

The meaning

The meaning

What Madhhab means in Islam

The plural is madhahib, and the word means a way gone along. Four Sunni schools survive with a living scholarly tradition: Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali. They agree on the pillars and on the great majority of rulings, and differ mostly in method and in detail. A madhhab is not a sect, and a Hanafi and a Shafi'i pray behind one another without difficulty.
