الجَرْحُ وَالتَّعْدِيل

Jarh wa Ta'dil

The discipline of criticising or vouching for narrators, so that a chain can be judged fairly.

What Jarh wa Ta'dil means in Islam

Jarh means to wound, ta'dil to declare upright. Scholars used a graded vocabulary, from thiqah thiqah at the top down to kadhdhab at the bottom, and applied it to teachers, friends and family without flinching. The working rule is that a criticism which explains itself outweighs a general word of praise, and that a critic known for harshness is read with that in mind. This was not backbiting; it was quality control on what people were told Allah's Messenger said.

  • Ilm al-RijalThe biographical science of the narrators: who they were, whom they met, and how reliable each was.عِلْمُ الرِّجَال
  • ThiqahA trustworthy narrator: upright in character and accurate in memory, the two conditions held together.ثِقَة
  • MajhulAn unknown narrator, whose identity or whose reliability the scholars could not establish.المَجْهُول
  • MatrukA report from a narrator the scholars abandoned, usually one accused of lying or of gross error.المَتْرُوك
