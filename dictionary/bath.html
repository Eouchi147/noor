Ba'th

The resurrection: bodies raised and souls returned, so that everyone stands for judgement.

Also written · Bath · Baath · Ba'ath · Yawm al-Bath · Resurrection · Raising of the dead · Nushur · Hashr · Mahshar · The gathering

The meaning

The meaning

What Ba'th means in Islam

The Qur'an answers the objection directly, asking who will give life to bones after they have crumbled, and replying that He who produced them the first time will (Qur'an 36:78-79). The gathering that follows is called the hashr, on an open plain where nobody is missed or overlooked. Sunni belief is that the raising is bodily and not only spiritual.
