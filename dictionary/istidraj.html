الاِسْتِدْرَاج

Istidraj

Being drawn in slowly by blessings while sinning, so that ease becomes a punishment rather than approval.

Also written · Istidraaj · Estidraj · Istidrag · Gradual lure · Being drawn in · Respite in sin

The meaning

The meaning

What Istidraj means in Islam

The Qur'an says of those who deny its signs: We will lead them on by degrees from where they do not know (Qur'an 7:182). It is the answer to a question people ask honestly, which is why the wrongdoer prospers. Comfort is not a verdict, and neither is hardship.
