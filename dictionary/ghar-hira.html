Ghar Hira

The small cave above Makkah where the Prophet was praying when revelation first came.

Also written · Cave of Hira · Hira · Hera · Ghar e Hira · Jabal an-Nur · Jabal al-Nour · Mountain of Light · Ghar Hera · Hira cave · Gar Hira · Hiraa

The meaning

What Ghar Hira means in Islam

It sits near the top of Jabal an-Nur, the mountain of light, a hard climb even now. He used to withdraw there for nights at a time before prophethood, and it was there that Jibril pressed him and said Read. He came home shaking and asked to be covered, and Khadijah wrapped him and told him Allah would never disgrace him. He is not reported to have returned to the cave afterwards, so climbing it is a matter of history, not a rite.
