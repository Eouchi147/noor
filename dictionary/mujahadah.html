المُجَاهَدَة

Mujahadah

The struggle against your own self: making yourself do the right thing when you do not want to.

Also written · Mujahada · Mujahadat al-nafs · Jihad al-nafs · Jihad an-nafs · Mojahada · Struggle against the self · Fighting the ego · Greater jihad · Inner jihad · Self-discipline

The meaning

The meaning

What Mujahadah means in Islam

The Qur'an promises guidance to those who strive for His sake (Qur'an 29:69). Classical writers describe it as three moves: opposing the appetite, refusing the whisper, and putting the limbs through the act until it becomes a habit. The often quoted line about returning from the lesser jihad to the greater jihad was judged by hadith scholars to have no sound chain, which does not remove the idea itself, since sound texts state it in other words.
