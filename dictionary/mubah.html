Mubah

Neutral: neither rewarded nor blamed, permitted and left entirely to your choice.

Also written · Mubaah · Mobah · Jayiz · Ibahah · Ibaha · Jaiz · Jaa'iz · neutral · permissible · indifferent · allowed but not rewarded

The meaning

What Mubah means in Islam

Which colour you wear and what you eat for breakfast are mubah, and most of ordinary life sits inside this category. A mubah act can become worship through intention, since eating so as to have strength to pray is rewarded, and sleeping so as to rise for fajr is rewarded. This is where the maxim that matters are judged by their intentions does its quietest and most useful work.
