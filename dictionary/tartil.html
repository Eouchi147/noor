التَّرْتيل

Tartil

Reciting slowly and distinctly, giving every letter its right, as the Qur'an itself instructs.

Also written · Tarteel · Tartil Quran · Tarateel · Tartil recitation · slow recitation · measured recitation · Tarteel Quran · Tartil e Quran · Tartila

The meaning

The meaning

What Tartil means in Islam

The command is direct: and recite the Qur'an with tartil. Ali is reported to have explained it as giving the letters their due and knowing where to stop. It was the Prophet's own pace, and it is the one to use if you want to understand what you are saying.
