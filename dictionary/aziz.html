العَزِيز

Aziz

A hadith which at no stage of its chain has fewer than two narrators.

Also written · Azeez · Aziiz · Aziz hadith · Azeez hadeeth · Two narrators · Aaziz · Azeez hadith · Aziz hadeeth · Azeez hadis

The meaning

The meaning

What Aziz means in Islam

The word here means scarce, hard to come by. Aziz sits between gharib, which has a single narrator somewhere in the chain, and mashhur, which has three or more. The category counts how many routes exist; it does not by itself say whether the hadith is sound.

Words that sit beside it

  • GharibA hadith narrated by only one person at some point in its chain.الغَرِيب
  • MashhurA hadith carried by three or more narrators at each stage, well known but short of mutawatir.المَشْهُور
  • MutawatirA report carried at every stage by so many people that they could not all have agreed on a lie.المُتَوَاتِر
  • AhadAny hadith that does not reach the level of mutawatir, however strong its single chain may be.الآحَاد
