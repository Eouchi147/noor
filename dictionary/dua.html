Du'a

Asking Allah directly, in any language, at any time, for anything permitted.

Also written · Dua · Duaa · Du'aa · Dooa · Doa · Supplication · Prayer · Invocation · Making dua · Adiyah · Ad'iyah · Asking Allah

The meaning

What Du'a means in Islam

The Qur'an says call on Me and I will answer you (Qur'an 40:60), and the Prophet peace be upon him said du'a is worship itself, in Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi. Scholars describe three possible answers: the thing asked for, something equal or better kept back for later, or a harm turned away. He also warned against impatience, saying a servant is answered as long as he does not say I asked and was not answered, in Bukhari and Muslim.
