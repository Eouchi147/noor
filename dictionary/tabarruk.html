Tabarruk

Seeking blessing through a person, place or object, which scholars permit narrowly and dispute widely.

Also written · Tabaruk · Tabaruck · Tabarruk bil-athar · Seeking blessing · Barakah from relics · Relics · Blessing from the righteous · Visiting graves for blessing · Touching the Kaaba for barakah

The meaning

The meaning

What Tabarruk means in Islam

The Companions sought blessing through the person of the Prophet peace be upon him, his hair and the water left from his wudu, which is established in Bukhari and Muslim. Everyone agrees that the blessing itself comes from Allah alone. Scholars disagree about extending the practice after his death to graves, relics and living righteous people, some permitting it within limits, others treating it as a door that opens onto shirk. All sides agree that asking the object itself for anything is not tabarruk but worship of it.
