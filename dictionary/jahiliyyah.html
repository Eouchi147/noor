الجَاهِلِيَّة

Jahiliyyah

The age of ignorance in Arabia before Islam, and any conduct that still belongs to it.

Also written · Jahiliya · Jahilia · Jaahiliyyah · Jahiliyya · Jahalat · Age of Ignorance · Days of Ignorance · Cahiliye · Jahiliyah · Pre-Islamic Arabia · Jahiliyyat

The meaning

The meaning

What Jahiliyyah means in Islam

The root is jahl, which means recklessness and hot headed ignorance rather than illiteracy. The Arabs of that age had poetry, memory, hospitality and law, so the word is a verdict on their worship and their violence, not on their intelligence. It also names a condition rather than only a period: the Prophet told Abu Dharr, after he insulted a man over his mother, that he was a man with jahiliyyah still in him.
