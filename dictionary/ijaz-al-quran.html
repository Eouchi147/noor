I'jaz al-Qur'an

The Qur'an's inimitability: that no one can produce its like, and the standing challenge to try.

Also written · Ijaz · Ijaz al-Quran · Ejaz · Ijazul Quran · Tahaddi · inimitability · miracle of the Quran · challenge of the Quran · scientific miracles · Ijaz ul Quran

The meaning

The meaning

What I'jaz al-Qur'an means in Islam

The challenge is made in stages within the Qur'an, from producing ten surahs like it down to a single surah, addressed to the finest speakers of a language that prized poetry above everything. Classical scholars located the miracle mainly in the arrangement and cadence of the Arabic, not only in what is said. Modern claims about scientific miracles are a separate and much more contested argument.
