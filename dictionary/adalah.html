العَدَالَة

Adalah

The uprightness of a narrator: sane, adult, Muslim, not openly sinful, not known for shameful habits.

Also written · Adala · Adaalah · Adalat · Adaalat · Adl · Aadalah · Uprightness · Adil · Adalah of narrator

The meaning

The meaning

What Adalah means in Islam

Adalah is about character, not perfection. A narrator does not need to be a saint, but he must not be known for lying, for open sin, or for behaviour suggesting he does not care what people think of him. It is one half of being thiqah. The other half is dabt.
