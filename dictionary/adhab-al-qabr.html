Adhab al-Qabr

The punishment or ease a person meets in the grave, before the Day of Judgement arrives.

Also written · Azab al-Qabr · Adhab ul Qabr · Azab e Qabr · Qabr azab · Punishment of the grave · Torment of the grave · Fitnat al-Qabr · Grave punishment · Trial of the grave

The meaning

The meaning

What Adhab al-Qabr means in Islam

The Qur'an describes the people of Fir'awn exposed to the Fire morning and evening before the Hour comes (Qur'an 40:46), and many sound hadiths describe the grave as either a garden of Paradise or a pit of the Fire. Ahl al-Sunnah affirm it as real without claiming to know how it works upon a body that has decayed or was never buried. The Mu'tazila denied it; the Sunni schools do not.

Evidence · Sunnah · Established in the authentic Sunnah
