Barakah

Blessing: a quality Allah puts in a thing so that a small amount goes further than it should.

Also written · Baraka · Barakat · Barkat · Barakaat · Mubarak · Tabarak · Blessing · Blessings · Barakah in time · Barakah in rizq · Barakah in wealth · Losing barakah

The meaning

What Barakah means in Islam

The Qur'an speaks of blessings opened from the sky and the earth for a town that believes (Qur'an 7:96), and quotes Isa saying he was made blessed wherever he is (Qur'an 19:31). Barakah attaches to time, food, wealth, knowledge and people, and it is not measured in quantity: an hour with barakah does the work of three. The Sunnah connects it with naming Allah before eating, honesty in trade, early mornings, and gratitude.
