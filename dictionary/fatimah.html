فَاطِمَة الزَّهْرَاء

Fatimah

The Prophet's youngest daughter, wife of Ali and mother of Hasan and Husayn.

What Fatimah means in Islam

She lived plainly; the famous exchange in which she asked her father for a servant and was taught words of remembrance instead is well known. He said she was a part of him, and that whoever angers her angers him. In his final illness he whispered something that made her weep and then something that made her smile: that he was dying, and that she would be the first of his household to follow him. She died about six months later, in her twenties.
