Tafsir bi'l-Ma'thur

Tafsir built on transmitted reports, as against tafsir bi'r-ra'y, which reasons from language and principle.

Also written · Tafsir bil Mathur · Tafsir bil-ma'thoor · Tafseer bil mathoor · Tafsir bir-ray · Tafsir bil ray · Tafsir bil rai · narration based tafsir · opinion based tafsir

The meaning

What Tafsir bi'l-Ma'thur means in Islam

Transmitted tafsir explains the Qur'an by the Qur'an, then by the Sunnah, then by the Companions and their students. Reasoned tafsir uses grammar, context and legal principle; it is praised when it is disciplined and condemned when it is a person reading their preferences into the text. Most of the great commentaries mix the two rather than holding to one.
