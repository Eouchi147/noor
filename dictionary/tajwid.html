Tajwid

The rules for pronouncing the Qur'an correctly: each letter from its place, with its proper qualities.

Also written · Tajweed · Tadjwid · Tajvid · Tajwed · Tejwid · Tajweed rules · Quran pronunciation · recitation rules · Tecvid · Tejvid

The meaning

The meaning

What Tajwid means in Islam

The word means to do a thing well. In practice it covers articulation points, letter qualities, lengthening, merging, hiding, and where to stop. Scholars broadly agree that learning enough to avoid errors that change meaning is required of every reciter; how much of the finer detail is obligatory is debated, and a beginner is not sinful for reciting imperfectly while learning.
