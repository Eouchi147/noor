The meaning

What Qur'an means in Islam

The word means the recitation, from a root meaning to read aloud. It is one book of 114 surahs, revealed piece by piece between Makkah and Madinah, memorised by the Companions and written down in the Prophet's lifetime. Muslims hold that the Arabic wording is itself the revelation, which is why any rendering into another language is called a translation of the meaning and never the Qur'an.

Words that sit beside it

  • MushafA physical copy of the Qur'an: the pages, ink and binding, as distinct from the revelation itself.المُصْحَف
  • SurahA chapter of the Qur'an; there are 114, arranged in the mushaf rather than by date of revelation.سورَة
  • AyahA verse of the Qur'an, and in ordinary Arabic a sign.آيَة
  • Nuzul al-Qur'anThe sending down of the Qur'an: gradually, over roughly twenty three years, alongside real events.نُزولُ القُرْآن
  • TarjamahA translation of the Qur'an, properly called a translation of the meanings rather than the Qur'an itself.تَرْجَمَةُ القُرْآن

More from The Qur’an

  • Al-Ahruf as-Sab'ahThe seven modes in which the Qur'an was revealed, an ease for the community; what a mode is stays disputed.الأَحْرُفُ السَّبْعَة
  • Hafs an AsimThe reading found in most printed mushafs today, transmitted by Hafs from his teacher Asim of Kufa.حَفْصٌ عَنْ عاصِم
  • IsrailiyyatStories from earlier scriptures that entered tafsir to supply details the Qur'an deliberately leaves out.الإِسْرائيليّات
  • Makharij al-HurufThe points in the mouth, throat and nose where each Arabic letter is produced.مَخارِجُ الحُروف
  • Nun Sakinah and TanwinA nun carrying no vowel, or a tanwin ending, which triggers four tajwid rules depending on what follows.النّونُ السّاكِنَةُ وَالتَّنْوين
  • Sifat al-HurufThe qualities of each letter: whispered or voiced, heavy or light, tense or flowing.صِفاتُ الحُروف
