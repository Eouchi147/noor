إِحْرام

Ihram

The sanctified state a pilgrim enters with intention and talbiyah, marked by simple unsewn dress.

Also written · Ehram · Ihraam · Ahram · Ihram clothes · Ihram state · two white cloths · Muhrim · Ihram garments

The meaning

The meaning

What Ihram means in Islam

The root means to make something forbidden, because entering it makes ordinary permissible things off limits. Men wear two unsewn white cloths; women wear ordinary modest clothing with the face and hands uncovered, and there is no special ihram dress for them. The common misunderstanding is that the towels are the ihram; they are not, and a person who has made the intention is in ihram whatever they happen to be wearing.

Evidence · Sunnah · Established in the authentic Sunnah
