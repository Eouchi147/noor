السِّيرَة

Sirah

The life story of the Prophet Muhammad, told in order, from birth to death.

Also written · Seerah · Sira · Seera · Siera · Sirah Nabawiyyah · Seerat · Sirat an-Nabi · Life of the Prophet · Prophetic biography · Seerah Nabawiyyah · Siyar · Sirah of Muhammad

The meaning

What Sirah means in Islam

The root means to travel, so a sirah is the road a person walked. The oldest surviving account is Ibn Ishaq's, preserved in the edition of Ibn Hisham, alongside al-Waqidi and the Tabaqat of Ibn Sa'd. Sirah writers accepted material on a looser standard of proof than the hadith scholars did, so a story being famous in the sirah does not by itself make it authentic.
