Ya'juj wa Ma'juj

Two peoples held back behind a barrier built by Dhu al-Qarnayn, released as a major sign of the Hour.

Also written · Yajuj Majuj · Yajooj Majooj · Yajooj wa Majooj · Yaajooj · Gog and Magog · Gog · Magog · Yajuj wa Majuj

The meaning

What Ya'juj wa Ma'juj means in Islam

The Qur'an tells the story of the barrier of iron and molten copper (Qur'an 18:94-98) and says that when they are released they will swarm down from every height (Qur'an 21:96). Scholars have guessed for centuries about who and where they are; the honest answer is that the texts describe a time, not a place on a map. Their release is one of the ten major signs.
