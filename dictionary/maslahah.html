The meaning

What Maslahah means in Islam

Its full name is al-maslahah al-mursalah, the untethered benefit, meaning one that revelation neither commands nor forbids by name. Registering marriages, compiling the Qur'an into one volume and building prisons were all defended this way. The schools differ over how far it may reach: Malikis and Hanbalis use it most freely, Shafi'is more narrowly, and all of them insist it can never overturn a clear text.
