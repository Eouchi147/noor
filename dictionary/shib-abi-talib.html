شِعْبُ أَبِي طَالِب

Shi'b Abi Talib

The Makkan valley where the Prophet's clan was penned by a boycott for about three years.

Also written · Shib Abi Talib · Shiab Abi Talib · Valley of Abu Talib · The Boycott · Social boycott of Banu Hashim · Shi'b Bani Hashim · Sheb Abi Talib · Shuab Abi Talib · Makkan boycott · Year of Sorrow · Am al-Huzn

The meaning

What Shi'b Abi Talib means in Islam

Quraysh hung a written pact in the Ka'bah agreeing to no trade and no marriage with Banu Hashim until they surrendered him. Believer and unbeliever of the clan were shut in together, living on what sympathisers smuggled past the guards, and children's crying could be heard outside. It broke when several Quraysh who had never been comfortable with it tore the document up. Khadijah and Abu Talib both died soon after, in what is called the Year of Sorrow.
