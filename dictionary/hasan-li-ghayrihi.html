الحَسَنُ لِغَيْرِهِ

Hasan li-ghayrihi

A weak hadith raised to the level of hasan because other chains carry the same meaning.

Also written · Hasan li ghayrihi · Hasan li ghairih · Hasan lighairihi · Hasan bi ghayrihi · Hasan due to others · Hasan li ghayrih · Hasan lighayrihi · Hasan li ghairihi · Hassan li ghayrihi

What Hasan li-ghayrihi means in Islam

This only works when the weakness is mild, such as a narrator with a poor memory, and never when a narrator is accused of lying. Two or three light chains leaning on one another can hold a report up. Scholars differ over how much support is enough, which is why the same hadith is sometimes graded hasan by one and weak by another.

Words that sit beside it

  • Da'ifA hadith that fails one of the conditions of soundness, through a broken chain or a faulty narrator.الضَّعِيف
  • HasanA hadith meeting every condition of sahih except that one narrator's accuracy falls slightly short.الحَسَن
  • Mutaba'ahA second chain supporting the first through the same Companion, showing a narrator was not alone.المُتَابَعَة
  • ShahidA supporting report of the same meaning narrated through a different Companion.الشَّاهِد
  • I'tibarThe search through the books for other chains of a hadith, to see whether anything supports it.الاعْتِبَار
