حَوْل

Hawl

The full lunar year that wealth must be held above the nisab before zakat falls due on it.

Also written · Haul · Hol · Howl · zakat year · lunar year for zakat · Hawl al-zakat · one year condition

The meaning

The meaning

What Hawl means in Islam

You set a date, usually a day in Ramadan for ease of memory, and pay on whatever qualifying wealth you hold on that date each year. Hanafis fold money that comes and goes during the year into that one date; the other schools start a fresh year for wealth newly acquired. Crops are the exception: their zakat is due at harvest, and buried treasure is due at once.

Evidence · Sunnah · Established in the authentic Sunnah
