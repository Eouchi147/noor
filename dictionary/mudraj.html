المُدْرَج

Mudraj

A hadith with words inserted into it, usually a narrator's explanation that later sounded like the Prophet's speech.

Also written · Mudrej · Idraj · Mudraj hadith · Mudraj al-matn · Mudraj al-isnad · Interpolated hadith · Inserted words · Idrag

The meaning

The meaning

What Mudraj means in Islam

Insertions happen in the text and, less often, in the chain. The usual sign is a phrase that explains an unfamiliar word, or that reads like a legal clause in the middle of plain speech. Scholars caught them by finding other versions where those same words appear separately as the narrator's own comment.
