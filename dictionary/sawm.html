Sawm

Fasting: abstaining from food, drink and intimacy from true dawn until sunset, with intention.

Also written · Siyam · Saum · Soum · Roza · Rozah · Oruc · Puasa · fasting · fast · Sawm Ramadan

The meaning

The meaning

What Sawm means in Islam

The word means to hold back. Fasting the month of Ramadan is the fourth pillar, and voluntary fasts are kept on other days through the year. The Qur'an sets its purpose as taqwa, God consciousness, and the Prophet warned that a fast without leaving off false speech is only hunger and thirst. Illness, travel, pregnancy, nursing, menstruation and old age all carry recognised concessions.
