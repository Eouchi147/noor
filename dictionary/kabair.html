What Kaba'ir means in Islam

The Qur'an promises that if you avoid the major sins you are forbidden, the smaller ones will be covered over (Qur'an 4:31). The Prophet peace be upon him named seven destructive sins in Bukhari and Muslim, among them shirk, killing a soul unjustly, and consuming interest. Scholars differ over the exact definition and therefore over the count, with classical books listing anywhere from seven to seventy and beyond. Minor sins turn serious when a person settles into them without concern.
