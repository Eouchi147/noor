إِبْلِيس

Iblis

The jinn who refused to prostrate to Adam out of pride, and was expelled and granted respite.

Also written · Iblees · Ibless · Ibliss · Eblis · Azazil · Lucifer · Satan · The devil

The meaning

The meaning

What Iblis means in Islam

The Qur'an tells the scene several times and states plainly that he was of the jinn, and so he departed from the command of his Lord (Qur'an 18:50). He asked for time until the Day they are raised and was given it. Iblis is his name, while shaytan describes what he became, and what his followers among jinn and men are called.
