الكُرْسِيّ

Kursi

Something vast that extends over the heavens and the earth, which most scholars hold is distinct from the Throne.

Also written · Kursee · Kursiyy · Al-Kursi · Footstool · The Chair · Kursi of Allah · Ayat al-Kursi · Aayatul Kursi

The meaning

The meaning

What Kursi means in Islam

The Qur'an says His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and preserving them does not tire Him (Qur'an 2:255). Ibn Abbas is reported to have explained it as the place of the two feet, while others read it as His knowledge or His dominion. A narration compares the seven heavens beside the Kursi to a ring thrown into open desert, and the Kursi beside the Throne in the same way.
