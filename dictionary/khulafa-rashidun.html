Khulafa Rashidun

The four caliphs after the Prophet: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali.

The meaning

What Khulafa Rashidun means in Islam

Rashid means guided along a straight road. Their rule ran from the Prophet's death in the eleventh year of the Hijrah to the killing of Ali in the fortieth, close to thirty years. The Prophet told his community to hold to his sunnah and the sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after him, which is why their practice carries a weight no later ruler's has. The title is Sunni usage; Shia hold that the succession belonged to Ali from the start. Some scholars add the six months of Hasan ibn Ali to complete the thirty years.
