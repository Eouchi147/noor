Qira'at

The canonical ways of reciting the Qur'an, each carried by chain from the Prophet through a named reader.

The meaning

What Qira'at means in Islam

Ten readings are accepted as authentic, each passed on by two main transmitters, giving twenty transmissions in common use. The differences are small and mostly touch vowels, lengthening or a single letter; they rarely change meaning, and where they do the two meanings sit alongside each other rather than clash. A reading counts as authentic only if it has a sound chain, fits the Uthmanic skeleton and is valid Arabic.
