المُتَوَاتِر

Mutawatir

A report carried at every stage by so many people that they could not all have agreed on a lie.

Also written · Mutawaatir · Mutewatir · Mutawatur · Mutawaatirah · Tawatur · Tawaatur · Mass transmitted · Mutawatir hadith · Mutawatir lafzi · Mutawatir manawi

What Mutawatir means in Islam

There is no fixed minimum number; what matters is that the crowd is wide enough and independent enough to rule out collusion. Two kinds are described: mutawatir in wording, where the same phrase comes through, and mutawatir in meaning, where many different reports converge on one point. The five daily prayers and the number of their units reach us this way, by continuous practice as much as by text, which is a stronger route than any single chain.
