عَرَفَة

Arafah

The open plain east of Makkah where pilgrims gather on the ninth of Dhul Hijjah.

Also written · Arafat · Arafa · Mount Arafat · Jabal ar-Rahmah · day of Arafah · Yawm Arafah · Arefe · Arafaat

The meaning

The meaning

What Arafah means in Islam

The small hill within it is called Jabal ar-Rahmah, the mount of mercy, and the farewell sermon was delivered nearby. Its day is the climax of the hajj. For Muslims not on hajj, fasting the day of Arafah is a strongly recommended sunnah described as expiating the year before and the year after; pilgrims themselves do not fast it.
