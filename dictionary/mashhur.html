المَشْهُور

Mashhur

A hadith carried by three or more narrators at each stage, well known but short of mutawatir.

Also written · Mashoor · Mashhoor · Meshhur · Mashhour · Mustafid · Mustafeed · Famous hadith · Mashhur hadith · Mashhoor hadeeth

The meaning

The meaning

What Mashhur means in Islam

The word simply means famous, and there is a trap in it. People also say mashhur for a saying famous on everybody's tongue, and many of those have weak chains or none at all. Fame among the public and fame in the chains are two different things.

Words that sit beside it

  • MutawatirA report carried at every stage by so many people that they could not all have agreed on a lie.المُتَوَاتِر
  • AzizA hadith which at no stage of its chain has fewer than two narrators.العَزِيز
  • GharibA hadith narrated by only one person at some point in its chain.الغَرِيب
  • La asla lahuA verdict meaning the saying has no chain at all, so it cannot even be graded.لَا أَصْلَ لَهُ
