Imam Abu Hanifah

The earliest of the four imams, a Kufan merchant whose school is the most widely followed today.

What Imam Abu Hanifah means in Islam

He was born around the year 80 and died in 150, in Baghdad. He is often counted among the Tabi'un on the basis that he may have seen the Companion Anas ibn Malik as a child, though scholars discuss this. He taught by argument in a circle rather than by dictation, and his students Abu Yusuf and Muhammad ash-Shaybani wrote the school down. He refused the post of chief judge and died in prison, and he is remembered as much for that refusal as for his law.
