Sabr

Holding yourself steady under what is hard, without complaint to creation and without giving up.

Also written · Sabar · Saber · Sabir · Sabrun · Sabr jameel · Sabr-e-jameel · Patience · Perseverance · Endurance · Being patient · Steadfastness

The meaning

The meaning

What Sabr means in Islam

The root means to restrain or to confine. Scholars usually give it three fields: steadiness in obeying Allah, steadiness in staying away from what He forbade, and steadiness under what He decrees. The Qur'an pairs it with prayer as the two things to seek help by (Qur'an 2:153). It does not mean feeling nothing: the Prophet peace be upon him wept when his son died and said the eye weeps and the heart grieves, but we say only what pleases our Lord, in Bukhari.
