الإِظْهار

Izhar

Pronouncing a nun sakinah or tanwin clearly, with no nasal hum, before one of six throat letters.

Also written · Izhaar · Idhar · Ith-har · Ezhar · Izhar halqi · Izhaar halqi · Izhar shafawi · clear pronunciation

The meaning

The meaning

What Izhar means in Islam

The six are hamzah, ha, ayn, ha, ghayn and kha, all produced in the throat. Because the throat sits far from the nose, the nun is simply left as it is. There is a matching rule for a silent meem, called izhar shafawi, made at the lips.
