السُّنَنُ الرَّواتِب

Sunnah Rawatib

The regular voluntary prayers attached before or after the five obligatory ones.

Also written · Sunan rawatib · Rawatib · Ratibah · Sunnah muakkadah · Sunnat · Sunnah prayers · Nafl · Nafil · Nawafil · voluntary prayers

The meaning

The meaning

What Sunnah Rawatib means in Islam

Twelve rak'ahs are reported as the standing pattern: two before Fajr, four before and two after Dhuhr, two after Maghrib, and two after Isha. The two before Fajr were the ones the Prophet never left. Any voluntary prayer outside this pattern is called nafl, and it carries no blame if missed.
