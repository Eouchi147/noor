Shubuhat

Doubtful matters: the grey band between what is clearly lawful and what is clearly forbidden.

The meaning

What Shubuhat means in Islam

The Prophet peace be upon him said the lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear, and between them are unclear matters that many people do not know, and whoever guards against them has protected his religion and his honour, in Bukhari and Muslim. The same hadith ends by naming the heart as the organ that settles the body. Leaving the doubtful is wara', and it is personal caution rather than a ruling you impose on other people.

Evidence · Sunnah · Established in the authentic Sunnah
