Khushu'

Stillness of the body and attention of the heart during prayer.

Also written · Khushu · Khushoo · Khusu · Khushuu · Kushu · concentration in prayer · focus in salah · humility in prayer

The meaning

The meaning

What Khushu' means in Islam

The Qur'an opens its description of successful believers with those who have khushu' in their prayer. It is not an emotion you must manufacture; it is mainly the practice of returning your attention each time it wanders, and of not fidgeting. Losing focus does not invalidate the prayer, which is why nobody should stop praying out of a feeling that their prayer is not good enough.
