تَكْبيرَةُ الإِحْرام

Takbirat al-Ihram

The opening "Allahu akbar" that begins the prayer and closes off everything outside it.

Also written · Takbir · Takbeer · Allahu akbar · Takbiratul ihram · opening takbir · Takbira · Takbeeratul ihram

The meaning

The meaning

What Takbirat al-Ihram means in Islam

Ihram here means entering a sanctified state, the same root as the pilgrim's ihram. Once you say the opening takbir, eating, speaking and turning away are no longer permitted until the closing salam. Smaller takbirs then mark each change of posture through the prayer.
