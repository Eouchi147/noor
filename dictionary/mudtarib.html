Mudtarib

A hadith narrated in versions that clash, with no way to prefer one over the other.

The meaning

What Mudtarib means in Islam

The word describes something shaking, unsteady on its feet. If one version can be shown to be stronger, the problem disappears and the weaker is simply set aside; idtirab is declared only when the versions are of equal weight and cannot be reconciled. It is a rare verdict, and it makes the hadith weak.
