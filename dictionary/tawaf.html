Tawaf

Circling the Kaaba seven times, keeping it on your left, starting and ending at the Black Stone.

Also written · Tawaaf · Tawwaf · Tavaf · Tauf · circumambulation · circling the Kaaba · Tawaf al-ifadah · Tawaf al-wada · Tawaf al-qudum · farewell tawaf

The meaning

The meaning

What Tawaf means in Islam

The Qur'an commands circling the Ancient House. Most schools require the pilgrim to be in a state of purity, and Hanafis count a tawaf made without wudu as valid but owing a dam. Each circuit begins and ends at the corner of the Black Stone. Hajj has up to three: tawaf al-qudum on arrival, tawaf al-ifadah after Arafah which is a pillar of the hajj, and tawaf al-wada as a farewell before leaving Makkah. There is no obligation to touch or kiss the stone; most pilgrims simply point to it.
