Sirat

The bridge laid over Hell that everyone must cross, at speeds matching their deeds.

Also written · Siraat · Sirath · Sarat · As-Sirat · Pul Sirat · Pul-e-Sirat · The Bridge · Bridge over Hell · Sirat al-Mustaqim

The meaning

What Sirat means in Islam

It is described in the long hadith of intercession in Bukhari and Muslim: people pass over it like lightning, like the wind, like a running horse, or crawling, and some are seized along the way. The Qur'an speaks of every one of you coming to it (Qur'an 19:71), which many commentators read as this crossing. The same word in Surah al-Fatihah means the straight path in this life, a different sense of one root.
