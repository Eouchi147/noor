المَتْرُوك

Matruk

A report from a narrator the scholars abandoned, usually one accused of lying or of gross error.

Also written · Matrook · Metruk · Matrook al-hadith · Matruk al-hadith · Abandoned narrator · Daif jiddan · Da'if jiddan · Dhaeef jiddan · Very weak hadith

The meaning

What Matruk means in Islam

Matruk sits just above fabricated. It is described as severely weak, da'if jiddan, and unlike ordinary weakness it cannot be repaired by supporting chains. When a book says a narrator is matruk al-hadith, the discussion is effectively over.
