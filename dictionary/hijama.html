Hijama

Cupping: suction on the skin with small incisions to draw blood out, practised and praised by the Prophet.

The meaning

What Hijama means in Islam

He was cupped, peace be upon him, and said that among the best of the remedies people use is cupping, in reports collected by Bukhari and Muslim. It is a recommended treatment, not an act of worship and not a cure for everything, and the reports naming particular days of the month are weighed differently by different scholars. It should be done by someone trained and clean. It is medicine, and the ordinary rules of medicine apply to it.
