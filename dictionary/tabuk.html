Tabuk

The last expedition the Prophet led, a long march north in year nine that ended without battle.

Also written · Battle of Tabuk · Ghazwat Tabuk · Jaysh al-Usrah · Army of Hardship · Tabook · Tebuk · Jang e Tabuk · Expedition of Tabuk · Tabuk expedition · Tabouk

What Tabuk means in Islam

It was called the army of hardship: high summer, scarce water, ripe harvests left behind, and a distant enemy. Uthman equipped a large part of it from his own wealth. No fighting took place. Its lasting story is the three Companions who simply failed to go and would not lie about why; the community was told to shun them for fifty nights until Surah at-Tawbah announced that Allah had turned to them, a passage that is really about honesty when a lie would have been easy.
