سورَة

Surah

A chapter of the Qur'an; there are 114, arranged in the mushaf rather than by date of revelation.

Also written · Sura · Soorah · Surat · Suratul · Suwar · Sourate · Sourat · Suras · chapter · Quran chapter · Sourah

The meaning

The meaning

What Surah means in Islam

Each surah has a name, usually taken from a striking word inside it, and every surah except at-Tawbah opens with the basmalah. They range from al-Baqarah at 286 verses down to al-Kawthar at three. The order in the mushaf is not the order of revelation, so many of the short surahs at the end are among the earliest to come down.

Words that sit beside it

  • AyahA verse of the Qur'an, and in ordinary Arabic a sign.آيَة
  • MushafA physical copy of the Qur'an: the pages, ink and binding, as distinct from the revelation itself.المُصْحَف
  • MakkiA verse or surah revealed before the migration to Madinah, whatever place it came down in.مَكِّيّ
  • Juz AmmaThe thirtieth and last part of the Qur'an, running from Surah an-Naba to the end.جُزْءُ عَمَّ
  • MufassalThe last stretch of the Qur'an, from about Surah Qaf to the end, made up of shorter surahs.المُفَصَّل

More from The Qur’an

  • Al-Ahruf as-Sab'ahThe seven modes in which the Qur'an was revealed, an ease for the community; what a mode is stays disputed.الأَحْرُفُ السَّبْعَة
  • Hafs an AsimThe reading found in most printed mushafs today, transmitted by Hafs from his teacher Asim of Kufa.حَفْصٌ عَنْ عاصِم
  • IsrailiyyatStories from earlier scriptures that entered tafsir to supply details the Qur'an deliberately leaves out.الإِسْرائيليّات
  • ManzilOne of seven divisions of the Qur'an, used by those who complete a full reading each week.مَنْزِل
  • QariA reciter of the Qur'an; in the classical sense, one trained and certified in a particular reading.القارِئ
  • Ta'wilInterpretation, and in a second sense the reality a statement finally turns out to refer to.التَّأْويل
