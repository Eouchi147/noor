'Urf

Local custom, which fiqh accepts as a decider where revelation has left a matter open.

The meaning

What 'Urf means in Islam

If a contract says a reasonable quantity, or a delivery in good time, it is 'urf that decides what those words mean. It cannot make lawful what the texts forbid; it fills gaps and defines undefined terms. Because customs change, rulings resting on 'urf change with them, which is why the same school can give different answers in different centuries or countries without contradicting itself.
