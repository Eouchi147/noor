A word of the Path

Zina الزِّنا

Qur’an

Sexual intercourse outside marriage: a major sin, and one of the fixed penalty offences.

What does Zina mean in Islam?

The Qur'an says do not go near it, calling it an immorality and an evil way, and the wording is read as forbidding the approaches to it as well as the act. The penalty carries a bar of proof so high it is almost never reached, four upright witnesses to the act itself, and the same passage punishes those who make the accusation without them. Islamic law discourages prying, and consistently prefers concealment and repentance to exposure.
