NOOR v70 · deploy these two files first if you want to test the prayer board

  masjid/setup.html      the wizard
  masjid/board.html      the board itself

Those two carry the whole Jumu'ah fix. Everything else in this zip is the rest of
v59 to v70 and can follow.

WHAT WAS WRONG, IN ORDER OF WHO IT BIT

1. The preview never reloaded. Changing only the # part of an iframe's address is
   not a navigation, so the browser kept the board it already had. The board reads
   its settings once, at startup. The preview therefore showed the empty board it
   loaded with, forever, while you typed. Every keystroke rewrote an address that
   nothing ever fetched.

2. The khutba fields would not accept a typed time. They were time pickers, and a
   time picker silently throws away anything it does not recognise. Typing
   1:15 PM and pressing enter left the field empty, with no message. The board then
   had no sitting to show, which looks identical to the feature not working.

3. The preview asked for the Friday screen through the address bar, which had to
   survive the redirect from board.html to board, and did not always.

WHAT TO EXPECT NOW

  Type 13:15, or 1:15 PM, or 1:15pm, or 2.45 pm. All four work.
  The preview redraws about a third of a second after you stop typing.
  If Jumu'ah is on and no time is set, the wizard says so in red.
  If a time cannot be read, it quotes your text back and shows what does work.
  The link for the television is /masjid/board, with no .html, so the screen
  stops taking a redirect every time it loads.
