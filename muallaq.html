A word of the Path

Mu'allaq المُعَلَّق

Editorial

A chain with its beginning cut off, where the author omits the narrators nearest to himself.

What does Mu'allaq mean in Islam?

The word means hung, as though the chain dangles with nothing above it. Al-Bukhari includes many mu'allaq reports in his Sahih, and scholars read them by their wording: those he states firmly, in the active form, are generally reliable and usually connected elsewhere in the book, while those he introduces with a passive form such as it is narrated are weaker. Ibn Hajar traced their full chains in a separate work.
